# Settings

Found under **Game Settings → Configure Settings → PF1e Revamp**. All are world settings (the GM sets them for everyone).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Apply energy resistance on quick damage** | On | PF1 only applies energy resistance and immunity through its Apply Damage dialog. When on, damage applied straight from a chat card is also reduced by the target's best matching resistance (or ignored if immune), and the target's owner and the GM get a private note of what was resisted. Turn off if another module already does this on quick damage. |
| **Shield bashes use martial weapon proficiency** | On | By the core rules, shields are martial weapons when used to bash, but PF1 gives every shield bash the −4 non-proficiency penalty. When on, characters with martial weapon proficiency bash without it. |
| **Variant channeling (optional rule)** | Off | *Ultimate Magic*'s variant channeling. When on, new clerics choose standard channel energy or a portfolio variant in the level-up window, and existing channelers can choose once from Channel Energy's note. See [Classes](Classes#variant-channeling-optional-rule). |

Everything else is on whenever its item is present. To stop an automation for one character, remove or disable the item (feature, trait or buff) as usual.
