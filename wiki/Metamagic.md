## Casting with metamagic

**Spontaneous casters** (sorcerer, oracle, bard…): PF1's casting window has a **Metamagic** section with a box for each metamagic feat the character has, a Heighten amount and an energy for Elemental Spell. It shows the slot level the casting will use (above 9th can't be cast), and casting spends that slot instead of the spell's own.

**Prepared casters** (wizard, cleric…): open the spell's sheet and use **Metamagic** at the top. Tick the feats; a copy ("Empowered Fireball") is prepared in the higher slot. Its save DC stays the spell's own (only Heighten raises it), and each casting of the copy applies its feats.

The spell's card lists the feats used and the slot spent. The module's own rolls follow the feats too: damage on later rounds, lasting areas' damage, *Call Lightning*'s bolts, temporary hit points and *Sleep*-style Hit Dice pools.

## The feats

| Feat | Slot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Extend | +1 | every duration the module applies is doubled (not concentration, instantaneous or permanent) |
| Empower | +2 | damage, healing and other rolled numbers ×1.5, including the level-based bonus |
| Maximize | +3 | rolled numbers at their maximum; with Empower, the maximum plus half a normal roll |
| Enlarge | +1 | close, medium and long range doubled (placing summons and attackers) |
| Widen | +3 | a burst, cone, emanation or spread's template twice as large (lines don't change) |
| Heighten | +1 to +9 | the spell counts as that much higher for save DCs |
| Quicken / Still / Silent | +4 / +1 / +1 | a swift action / no somatic / no verbal components, noted on the card |
| Reach | +1 per step | one range category longer; melee touch becomes ranged touch |
| Intensified | +1 | the cap on damage dice 5 levels higher |
| Persistent | +2 | a creature that saves must save again; failing the second, it takes the full effect |
| Selective | +1 | an instantaneous area spell: choose up to your casting ability's modifier of creatures to leave out |
| Dazing | +3 | a creature damaged and failing its save is dazed for the spell's original level in rounds; a spell without a save gets a Will save against the daze |
| Elemental | +1 | the damage becomes the chosen energy (acid, cold, electricity or fire) |
| Merciful | +0 | the damage is nonlethal |

Metamagic doesn't work on spell-like abilities (Paizo FAQ).

## Metamagic rods

Any caster's casting window lists the metamagic rods they carry that fit the spell's level (lesser: 3rd or lower, normal: 6th, greater: 9th), with the uses left today. Pick one (one rod per spell): its feat applies without raising the slot, and it combines with the caster's own metamagic feats. A rod's three uses a day come back when the character rests.

## With Nevela's Automation Suite

Nevela's Automation Suite has its own metamagic, with more feats, class features (arcanist, sorcerer, oracle, wizard, psychic, kineticist) and traits. When it is active, PF1e Revamp steps aside:
- **Casting:** use Nevela's **Metamagic** box in the casting window; PF1e Revamp's section, rods list and prepared-copy button are hidden. Nevela spends the slot and sets the save DC.
- **This module's features still follow it:** *Extend* doubles lasting areas, summons and attackers; *Empower* and *Maximize* raise damage on later rounds, lasting areas' damage and *Call Lightning*'s bolts; *Intensified* raises their dice caps; *Enlarge* and *Reach* lengthen placement ranges. The card says which feats it followed.
- **Left to Nevela:** *Dazing* and *Persistent* (its own save buttons on the card) and *Selective* (it removes the excluded creatures from the targets).
- **Not available while Nevela is active:** *Widen*, *Elemental* and *Merciful*, which Nevela doesn't have.

Metamagic copies prepared before Nevela was turned on keep working.

## Not covered yet

The 16 feats above are from the *Core Rulebook* and the *Advanced Player's Guide*. The other metamagic feats (about 70, from *Ultimate Magic* onward) can still be used, but their effects are applied by hand; they are planned.
