# Races

Racial traits come from **Pathfinder 1e Content** (`pf-content`).

## Choosing racial traits

**Before:** a PF1 race item applies the race's fixed numbers: ability modifiers, skill bonuses, size and speed, the human bonus feat. But its **traits aren't linked**. The Ifrit's fire resistance, darkvision and spell-like ability exist as separate items that had to be found and added by hand. The "+2 to one ability score of your choice" wasn't applied at all.

**Now:** dropping a race onto a character opens a racial-trait review:
- **Standard traits** are listed and pre-checked.
- **Alternate traits** are listed unchecked, each saying what it replaces ("replaces Energy Resistance"). Checking one unchecks what it replaces.
- Traits that let you **pick an ability score** get a dropdown.

Click **Apply racial traits** and the chosen traits are added to the character. For characters who already have a race, the race's note has a **Review racial traits…** button. Traits already on the sheet show as *(already on sheet)* and aren't duplicated.

### Nothing counts twice
Some trait items repeat a bonus the race item already gives. Human *Skilled* repeats the extra skill ranks, and *Bonus Feat* repeats the extra feat. Those traits are added for reference without their bonus, so the race's version is the only one that counts.

### Alternate traits remove what they replace
Picking an alternate removes the replaced trait. If the replaced trait's bonus lives on the race item, it's removed from your copy of the race too. For example, *Heart of the Mountains* replaces *Skilled*, so the race's extra skill ranks go.

### Undoing
Removing the race removes the traits it added. **Review again…** restores everything and lets you choose afresh.

## The "+2 to any ability" races
**Humans, Half-Elves and Half-Orcs** get a dropdown for which ability takes the +2. The choice can be changed by reviewing the racial traits again.

## Racial energy resistance
Traits that say *"you have fire resistance 5"* (and cold, acid, electricity or sonic) now grant **real resistance** that reduces damage. This covers the Ifrit, Aasimar, Tiefling, Oread, Sylph, Undine and similar races. It also works for racial trait items added by hand before installing the module.

Energy resistances **never stack**. A character with racial fire resistance 5 and fire resistance 10 from a bloodline has fire resistance **10**, not 15. See [Combat Rules](Combat-Rules#energy-resistance).

## Racial bonuses against effects
"+2 racial bonus vs. …" traits apply automatically when the save is rolled from the effect's chat card. That covers halfling *Fearless*, dwarf *Hardy*, elf and half-elf *Elven Immunities* (enchantment), and gnome *Illusion Resistance*. See [Combat Rules](Combat-Rules#save-bonuses-against-effects).

## Examples

**Ifrit:** fire resistance 5 (real), darkvision 60 ft, *Burning Hands* once per day as a spell-like ability, and Fire Affinity as a note. Alternates include *Wildfire Heart*: +4 initiative instead of fire resistance.

**Human:** choose the +2 ability. *Skilled* and *Bonus Feat* are counted once. Many alternates are available: *Heart of the Fey*, *Dual Talent* (two abilities), *Silver Tongued* and more.

**Elf and Half-Elf:** +2 against enchantment applies automatically against enchantment spells. The half-elf picks their +2 ability.
