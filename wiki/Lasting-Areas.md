# Lasting Areas

Spells whose area stays on the map for their duration keep working on the creatures in it: clouds and fogs, entangling plants, walls, spheres you steer, and storms that call down bolts.

## How it works

Cast the spell and place its template as usual. From then on the GM's game tracks the template for the spell's duration:
- **At the casting**, the creatures inside make the spell's save (or face its check), and the result is applied.
- **Moving in.** A creature that moves in later does the same. Some areas also act on a creature moving within them.
- **Every round**, on the caster's turn or at the start or end of each creature's own turn as the spell says, those inside save again or take the area's damage.
- **Leaving** ends what the area holds (entangled, grappled, a buff), except what the spell says lingers. *Stinking Cloud*'s nausea, for example, lasts 1d4+1 rounds more.
- **Ending.** When the duration has passed, the template is removed and everyone it held is released. Deleting the template by hand does the same, and lingering conditions still get their rounds.

Every change appears on a card listing what happened to whom, with an **Undo** for the GM. A summoned creature takes its turn with its summoner, so it saves or takes damage then.

Many spells state their area only in text in PF1's data, so PF1 offers no template (*Web*, *Fog Cloud*, *Stinking Cloud*, *Cloudkill*, *Solid Fog*). PF1e Revamp supplies one when the area is a fixed radius, a fixed line or a detect spell's cone. Areas that grow with caster level, cubes and shaped areas are placed by hand.

## Clouds and fogs

Fogs give concealment through the miss chances: 20% within 5 feet, 50% beyond, when either creature is in the fog or the fog lies between them. They are rolled with the other miss chances ([Spells and Buffs](Spells-and-Buffs#miss-chances)).

| Spell | What it does |
|---|---|
| *Fog Cloud*, *Obscuring Mist*, *Twilight Haze* | Fog. |
| *Barrow Haze* | Fog that doesn't hinder its own caster. |
| *Sleet Storm* | 50% concealment; moving needs Acrobatics (see below). |
| *Stinking Cloud* | Fog. Living creatures: Fortitude or nauseated while inside and 1d4+1 rounds after; a new save on the caster's turn for those who saved. Immune to poison: unaffected. |
| *Cloud of Seasickness* | As *Stinking Cloud*, sickened instead of nauseated. |
| *Drunkard's Breath* | As *Stinking Cloud*, without the fog. |
| *Euphoric Cloud* | Fog. Fortitude or fascinated while inside and 1d4+1 rounds after. |
| *Poisonous Cloud* | Fog. Fortitude or 1d2 Con damage on entering and on each of the caster's turns. |
| *Virulent Miasma* | Fog, with a reminder of its −4 on saves against disease. |
| *Cloudkill* | Fog. 3 Hit Dice or fewer: killed; 4–6: Fortitude or killed; more: 1d4 Con each round (Fortitude halves). Drifts. |
| *Acid Fog* | *Solid Fog*'s penalties and concealment, and 2d6 acid to everyone inside on the caster's turn. |
| *Incendiary Cloud* | Fog, and 6d6 fire (Reflex half) on the caster's turn. Drifts. |
| *Flashfire* | Concealment inside. Reflex or 1d6 fire per 3 caster levels and catching fire, on entering and at the start of each turn inside. |

## Entangling, grabbing and holding

| Spell | What it does |
|---|---|
| *Entangle*, *Thorny Entanglement* | Reflex or entangled; those who saved and stay save again at the end of the caster's turn. (The thorns' volleys are rolled by hand.) |
| *Sickening Entanglement* | As *Entangle*; also Fortitude or sickened on entering and at the end of each of the creature's turns, lasting 1d4 rounds after leaving. |
| *Burning Entanglement* | As *Entangle*; at the start of each turn inside, 4d6 fire (Reflex half) if entangled, 2d6 (Reflex negates) if not. Smoke conceals beyond 5 feet. |
| *Thirsting Entanglement* | As *Entangle*; each failed save also drains 1d2 Con. |
| *Web* | Reflex or grappled at the casting; moving in takes a combat maneuver or Escape Artist check against the spell's DC. |
| *Web Cloud* | Drifts; Reflex or grappled when it rolls over a creature, and at the start of each turn inside. The trapped stay trapped after it passes. |
| *Black Tentacles* | One grapple roll a round for everyone inside (caster level + 4 + 1); a hit grapples and deals 1d6+4. |
| *Impossible Angles* | Will or sickened (disoriented) while inside, with a new save each round. The random direction of the first 5 feet moved is up to the table. |

## Moving through difficult ground

| Spell | What it does |
|---|---|
| *Grease*, *Greater Grease* | Reflex or fall at the casting; moving in it takes DC 10 Acrobatics (failing stops the creature, and it saves or falls; failing by 5 or more is a fall). |
| *Sleet Storm* | The same check, without the save. |
| *Spike Growth*, *Spike Stones* | 1d4 (*Spike Stones* 1d8) piercing per 5 feet moved through it, then Reflex or half land speed for 24 hours. |
| *Sawtooth Terrain* | At the casting: Reflex or 3d8 piercing, 2d6 bleed and half speed for 24 hours. Then 1d8 piercing to any creature moving in. |
| *Create Pit* | At the casting: Reflex or fall in (1d6 per 10 feet of depth, prone). Walking in is a fall; ending a turn next to it is a Reflex save at +2. |

## While inside

Some areas put a buff on the creatures inside and take it away when they leave:
- *Solid Fog*: −2 on melee attacks and damage (with a reminder of half speed, no 5-foot step, no ranged weapon attacks).
- *Condensed Ether*: −2 AC and Reflex saves (half speed, no 5-foot step, ranged weapons useless, 20% miss chance for rays).
- *Ice Storm*, *Volcanic Storm*, *Gravel Vortex*: −4 Perception, and difficult terrain. Their damage is PF1's, once, at the casting.
- *Silence*: a reminder that spells with verbal components can't be cast.

## Walls

*Wall of Fire*, *Blade Barrier* and *Wall of Clockwork* place a line template (20 feet per caster level; *Wall of Clockwork* 5 feet per caster level). Shorten it, or change it to a circle for the ring form. A creature passes through the wall when its move crosses the line or ends in the wall's squares. Moving out of them isn't another crossing.

| Wall | Passing through | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| *Wall of Fire* | 2d6 + caster level (max +20) fire; double to undead | The same. Its heat hits creatures on the hot side (2d4 within 10 feet, 1d4 within 20) when it appears and on each of the caster's turns. The hot side faces away from the caster; the GM's card can switch it. |
| *Blade Barrier* | 1d6 per caster level (max 15d6), Reflex half | A Reflex save avoids it entirely; otherwise the full damage. |
| *Wall of Clockwork* | 1d6 per 2 caster levels (max 10d6), Reflex half | It can't be placed on a creature: place it again. |

Cover through a wall (+4 AC, +2 on Reflex saves) is reminded on the GM's card.

## Spheres you steer

*Flaming Sphere*, *Greater Flaming Sphere*, *Aggressive Thundercloud* and *Greater Aggressive Thundercloud* place a 5-foot sphere. Drag it into a creature's space to strike it:

| Sphere | Strike |
|---|---|
| *Flaming Sphere* | Reflex or 3d6 fire |
| *Greater Flaming Sphere* | Reflex or 6d6 fire and catching fire |
| *Aggressive Thundercloud* | Reflex or 3d6 electricity; concealment inside |
| *Greater Aggressive Thundercloud* | Reflex or 6d6 electricity; the first creature it damages is also stunned 1 round (Fortitude negates, by hand) |

A creature walking into a sphere isn't struck. The sphere stopping for the round, its speed and its light are left to the table.

## Call Lightning

*Call Lightning* and *Call Lightning Storm* put a **Call a bolt** button on the spell's card for the caster's owner and the GM, with the number of bolts left. Target the creatures in the bolt's square, tick **Outdoors in a storm** if it applies, and click. Each bolt gets one damage roll, and each creature a Reflex save for half. A card follows, with an Undo.

## Drifting clouds

*Cloudkill*, *Incendiary Cloud*, *Web Cloud* and *Plague Storm* move 10 feet at the start of the caster's turn, straight away from where the caster stood when casting. Then they act on the creatures in their new spread; creatures they roll over count as entering. A wall stops the cloud's center there, though the spread still flows around corners.

A cloud cast on the caster's own square has no direction: drag it once and it keeps going that way. You can drag a cloud at any time (concentration, wind, a slope), and it goes on from where you leave it.

## Areas and walls

A template is the most a spell's area can reach; the walls decide who it really catches. This applies to every spell's rows, not only lasting areas.
- **Bursts, emanations, cones and lines** need a clear straight line from the point of origin to a creature. They don't go around corners.
- **Spreads** (*Fireball*, *Web*, *Cloudkill*) do turn corners, but only as far as their radius measured around the walls.
- **Walls that block movement** count, and doors only while closed.

The kind of area is read from the spell's area text when its template is placed.

## Limits

- The map is flat: height isn't counted, so a flying creature above a cloud is still "inside".
- A square counts by its center. On a gridless scene, spreads use the straight-line rule.
- Wind, slopes and the spheres' and clouds' range limits are left to the GM.
- Areas that grow with caster level, cubes and shaped areas are placed by hand.
