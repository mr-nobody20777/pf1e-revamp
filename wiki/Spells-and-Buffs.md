# Spells and Buffs

PF1e Revamp recognizes the spell buffs from PF1 and **Pathfinder 1e Content** by name, so there's nothing special to use. Put the buff on the character as usual, and it now does what the spell says.

## Spells on their targets

**Before:** PF1 rolled a spell's attack and damage and put a save button on the card, but it never said who was affected, never checked spell resistance, and never applied what a failed save does.

**Now:** a spell's card lists every creature it affects (your targets, or the creatures in its template), with one row each:
- **Spell resistance** is checked for you: the caster level check (1d20 + caster level, +2 each for Spell Penetration, Greater Spell Penetration and Elven Magic) is rolled for every creature with SR. Everyone sees the roll and whether it got through; only the GM sees the SR value. A resisted spell has no effect on that creature.
- **Touch and ray spells** are compared with the creature's touch AC (a natural 20 always hits, a natural 1 always misses). Only the GM sees the AC. Many touch spells have no attack roll in PF1's data (Touch of Fatigue, Touch of Blindness); for a touch spell on a creature that isn't an ally (allies and the caster accept the touch, even of a harmful spell, but still save against one that isn't harmless), the module rolls the melee touch attack (base attack bonus, Strength or Dexterity with Weapon Finesse, size and attack bonuses). Mudball's ranged touch takes −4 for shooting into melee when the target is next to one of the caster's allies, unless the caster has Precise Shot.
- **Personal spells** (range: personal) go on the caster.
- **Saving throws** are rolled from the row: its owner (or the GM, for NPCs) clicks the save button. PF1's own save button works too: a save rolled against the card fills in the row. A natural 1 always fails and a natural 20 always succeeds.
- **The result is applied** as soon as it's known: conditions with their duration, the spell's buff at the caster's level for the spell's duration, or a penalty. The row says what was applied.
- **Harmless spells on allies** (Bless, Shield of Faith, Enlarge Person on a friend) are accepted: no spell resistance, no save.
- **Immunities** are found when the spell resolves, never shown beforehand: a creature that can't be affected says "unaffected" (the GM sees why). Mind-affecting spells don't affect undead, constructs, vermin, oozes, plants or mindless creatures; undead and constructs ignore Fortitude effects that aren't harmless; Hit Dice limits, creature types ("person" spells: humanoids), sightless creatures and condition immunities (elves and sleep, for example) are all checked.
- **Damage** stays with PF1's damage buttons; the row says whether the creature takes full or half.

Any spell with a save or spell resistance gets these rows. The spells below also apply their effect:

| Spells | Effect applied |
|---|---|
| Daze, Daze Monster | Dazed 1 round (humanoids of 4 HD or less; any living creature of 5 HD or less), then immune to that spell for 1 minute |
| Flare, Flare Burst | Dazzled 1 minute (not sightless or already dazzled creatures) |
| Touch of Fatigue, Litany of Weakness, Frostbite | Fatigued |
| Cause Fear | Frightened 1d4 rounds, or shaken 1 round on a successful save (5 HD or less, living) |
| Doom | Shaken 1 minute per level |
| Color Spray | By Hit Dice: up to 2 HD unconscious 2d4 rounds, then blinded and stunned 1d4 rounds, then stunned 1 round (only living creatures fall unconscious); 3-4 HD blinded and stunned 1d4 rounds, then stunned 1 round; 5 HD or more stunned 1 round |
| Sleep, Hypnotism | A pool of Hit Dice (4, or 2d4), fewest HD first, then those nearest the spell's origin; asleep, or fascinated |
| Lesser Confusion, Entangle, Grease, Vanish, Ear-Piercing Scream, Ray of Sickening | Confused, entangled, prone, invisible, dazed 1 round, sickened |
| Ray of Enfeeblement, Touch of Gracelessness | A Strength or Dexterity penalty (1d6 + 1 per two caster levels, maximum +5; halved on a save). The penalty doesn't stack with itself: a lower one doesn't replace a higher one. |
| Bless, Bane | The buff on the allies (Bless) or enemies (Bane) among the creatures in the area |
| Shield, Divine Favor, True Strike, Entropic Shield, Expeditious Retreat, Longstrider, Aspect of the Falcon, Lead Blades, Veil of Positive Energy, Adoration, Deadeye's Lore, Gravity Bow, Shock Shield | The buff on the caster, with or without targets |

**Save modifiers.** Hypnotism's +2 in combat (−2 for a single creature not in combat) and Hideous Laughter's +4 for a creature of another type than the caster are ticked entries in the save window, set for the situation.

**New saves to end an effect.** On the affected creature's turn in combat, a card offers its owner and the GM a new save:
- **Hideous Laughter**: once, on its next turn (a full-round action); failure means it lasts the full duration.
- **Mudball** (Reflex, at the start of its turn), **Shadow Trap** (a full-round action) and **Unlock Flesh** (a standard action): every turn until it succeeds, which ends the blindness, entanglement or staggering.

Notes the row adds for the GM: Entangle's saves in the area, Command's chosen command.

**Fixes to PF1's spell data:**
- **Magic Missile** rolls one 1d4+1 for every missile (two at 3rd level, up to five at 9th) and needs no attack: the row ignores the attack roll PF1 makes. The missiles can be split between targets; apply each part from the card.
- **Cure spells harm undead and inflict spells heal them**, as the spells say: the card's healing button deals the amount as damage to an undead creature, and its damage button heals one. Halve it for a successful Will save.
- **Disrupt Undead** only affects undead.

With **Nevela's Automation Suite** set to apply a buff for a spell's action, PF1e Revamp leaves that spell's buff to Nevela and only fills in the rows.

## Miss chances

**Before:** Blur, Displacement and similar buffs only printed a reminder; the miss chance had to be rolled by hand.

**Now:** when someone attacks a creature with one of these buffs, the miss chance is **rolled for every attack** and shown under each attack on the card. A red bar means the attack misses; green means it doesn't.

| Buff | Miss chance |
|---|---|
| **Blur** | 20% |
| **Displacement** | 50% |
| **Blink** | 50% against attacks; 20% if the attacker sees invisible creatures or uses a ghost touch or force attack; none if both. Single-target spells cast at a blinking creature have a 50% chance to fail. The blinker's own attacks have a 20% miss chance. |
| **Entropic Shield** | 20%, ranged attacks only |
| **Crystalline Dust** | 20% |
| Target is **invisible** (and the attacker can't see invisible) | 50% |
| Attacker is **blinded** | 50% |

The rules details are handled for you:
- **Miss chances don't stack**: only the highest one is rolled.
- **True seeing** negates Blur and Displacement; **see invisibility** doesn't, but does reduce Blink.
- **Blind-Fight** rerolls a concealment miss in melee. See [Feats](Feats#blind-fight).
- With **Nevela's Automation Suite**, its *Concealed* condition is left to Nevela. If a buff gives a higher miss chance, Nevela's roll is struck through so only one counts.

## Alignment wards

**Before:** pf-content's ward buffs either did nothing automatically or, for the auras, gave their bonus against **everyone**.

**Now:** Protection from Evil / Good / Chaos / Law (+2) and Holy Aura, Unholy Aura, Shield of Law and Cloak of Chaos (+4):
- **AC:** when a creature of the opposed alignment attacks, the target's AC on the attack card includes the deflection bonus. Hover it to see the breakdown.
- **Saves:** saves rolled against that creature's effects get the resistance bonus.
- **No stacking:** if the character already has a higher deflection or resistance bonus, nothing extra is added.
- The auras' always-on bonuses are removed from the character's copy, so they only apply against the right alignment.

## One-roll buffs

These are "on a single roll" bonuses, so the roll window shows a ticked box for each one (for example **☑ Guidance +1 (one roll)**, with `1[Guidance]` in the Situational Bonus field). Leave it ticked to use the bonus on this roll, or untick it to save it for a later one. Rolls made without a window (shift-click) use it automatically.

| Buff | What happens |
|---|---|
| **True Strike** | +20 on your next attack roll (the first attack only, if you make several), then the buff switches off. |
| **Guidance** | +1 on your next attack roll, saving throw or skill check, then it switches off. |
| **Bit of Luck** (Luck domain) | For a round, every d20 you roll is rolled twice and the better result kept. See [Classes](Classes#luck-domain-bit-of-luck). |
| **Prescience** (Foresight wizard) | A stored d20 you choose when to use. See [Classes](Classes#foresight-subschool-prescience). |

If you cancel a roll's dialog, the buff isn't used up.

## Buffs applied for you

Casting **Guidance**, or using **Bit of Luck** or **Resistant Touch**, puts the buff on your **target** (yourself if nobody is targeted; Resistant Touch needs an ally targeted). Recasting on someone who already has it switches it back on. If Nevela's Automation Suite is set to apply a buff for that action itself, PF1e Revamp lets Nevela do it.

## Save bonuses from buffs

Buffs that give a save bonus against a kind of effect apply it automatically when the save is rolled from that effect's chat card:
- **Remove Fear**: +4 morale vs fear
- **Death Ward**: +4 morale vs death spells and death effects
- **Mind Blank**: +8 resistance vs mind-affecting spells and effects, minus any resistance bonus you already have

Details and rules in [Combat Rules](Combat-Rules#save-bonuses-against-effects).

## Buff Library compendium

The module's **Buff Library** compendium has buffs not found in PF1 or pf-content: **Protection from Spells**, **Spider Climb** (climb speed 20 ft) and **True Strike**.
