## Two-weapon fighting

**Before:** PF1 has no two-weapon fighting at all. Penalties, the off-hand attack and its reduced Strength damage all had to be handled by hand.

**Now:** when you attack with a weapon, PF1's attack dialog has a **Two-weapon fighting** checkbox and an **Off-hand** picker listing your other weapons (including a shield you can bash with). With it checked:

| | Main hand | Off hand |
|---|---|---|
| No feat | −6 | −10 |
| Off-hand weapon is light | −4 | −8 |
| **Two-Weapon Fighting** | −4 (−2 with a light off-hand) | −4 (−2 with a light off-hand) |

- The **off-hand attack is rolled automatically** right after the main-hand attacks, as its own card, using **½ Strength** on damage.
- **Improved Two-Weapon Fighting** adds a second off-hand attack at −5, and **Greater Two-Weapon Fighting** a third at −10.
- **Double Slice**: the off-hand uses full Strength on damage.
- **Two-Weapon Defense**: +1 shield bonus to AC while fighting this way (+2 when fighting defensively or using total defense), shown as a buff that lasts until your next turn.
- **Two-Weapon Rend**: the off-hand card has a **Two-Weapon Rend** button. Click it when both weapons hit the same target, and it rolls the extra 1d10 + 1½ × Strength damage. It works once per round.
- The choice is remembered per weapon, so your usual off-hand is pre-selected next time.

Single attacks (not full attacks) are unaffected. Only melee and thrown weapons can be used.

## Shield bashes

**Before:** PF1 gives every shield bash the −4 non-proficiency penalty, even for characters proficient with martial weapons (which, by the rules, covers shield bashes).

**Now:**
- **The −4 is gone** for characters with martial weapon proficiency. This is a world setting, on by default; see [Settings](Settings).
- With the **Shield-Trained** trait, shield bashes are **simple** weapons for you and **heavy shields count as light weapons**. That matters for the two-weapon fighting penalty and for Weapon Finesse.
- Bashing with a shield in your off hand works with two-weapon fighting like any other weapon.

## Blind-Fight

When you attack a target with a miss chance from concealment (Blur, Displacement, invisibility, a blinded attacker), the miss chance is **rerolled once in melee** if the first roll misses. Both rolls are shown on the card. As the feat says, it doesn't help against Blink. See [Spells and Buffs](Spells-and-Buffs#miss-chances).

## Selective Channeling

When the cleric channels energy, the burst checklist lets you uncheck up to your **Charisma modifier** in other creatures. Without the feat, only the cleric can be left out. See [Classes → Channel Energy](Classes#channel-energy).

## Weapon Finesse

Works as normal in PF1, and also applies to **light shield bashes**, and to heavy shield bashes with Shield-Trained. You use Dex instead of Str to attack when it's higher, with the shield's armor check penalty applied, as the feat requires.
