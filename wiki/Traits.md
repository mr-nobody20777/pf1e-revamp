# Traits

Character traits come from **Pathfinder 1e Content** (`pf-content`). Many simple traits (e.g. *Reactionary*'s +2 initiative) already work in PF1, and PF1e Revamp leaves those alone. The traits below are the ones it changes.

## Traits that ask for a choice

These traits leave a choice open, and PF1's version never asked for it, so the bonus didn't apply. Now, adding one opens a small window to make the choice. The trait's note shows what you picked and has a **Change…** button.

| Trait | What you choose | What it does now |
|---|---|---|
| **Criminal** | Disable Device, Intimidate or Sleight of Hand | +1 trait bonus on that skill, and it's always a class skill |
| **Dilettante Artist** | A Perform category, and whether Diplomacy or Perform becomes a class skill | +1 trait bonus on Diplomacy and on that Perform category, plus the class skill |
| **Magical Knack** | A spellcasting class | +2 caster level in that class, but **never above your Hit Dice** (see below) |
| **Gifted Adept** | One of your spells | That spell is cast at **+1 caster level**, so its damage dice, duration and range scale as if you were a level higher |

### Magical Knack: why it may show +0
The trait reads: *"your caster level in that class gains a +2 trait bonus as long as this bonus doesn't increase your caster level higher than your current Hit Dice."* A single-class caster's caster level already equals their Hit Dice, so **the trait gives nothing until the character multiclasses**. A wizard 1 / fighter 2, for example, gets +2. PF1's version gave +2 regardless, and PF1e Revamp corrects that. The trait's note shows the current bonus.

## Traits that work automatically

| Trait | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Shield-Trained** | Light and heavy shield bashes count as **simple** weapons for you, and heavy shields count as **light** weapons: for two-weapon fighting, and for Weapon Finesse, which lets you use Dex to attack. See [Feats](Feats#shield-bashes). |
| **Swordlord's Page** | +1 on **critical confirmation rolls** with an edged (slashing or piercing) weapon you're proficient with. Only the confirmation roll gets it, not the attack. |
| **Student of Philosophy** | Diplomacy and Bluff chat cards show a **second total using your Intelligence modifier** (when it's higher than Charisma): *"With Int (Student of Philosophy; persuading only): 17"*. Use it when persuading someone or telling a lie. The trait doesn't apply to gathering information or feinting, so the normal total stays too. |
| **Lonely** (drawback) | −2 on saves against **charm** spells and spell-like abilities, applied automatically when you save from the effect's chat card. Other spells aren't affected. |
| Any trait with a "+X vs. …" save bonus | Applies automatically against matching effects. See [Combat Rules](Combat-Rules#save-bonuses-against-effects). |

## Traits that stay as reminders

Some traits are left as notes on purpose:

- **Rich Parents**: only affects starting gold.
- **Oppressive Expectations** and **Dependent**: they trigger when you *fail* a check, but most checks in play are rolled without a DC entered, so the module can't tell a failure from a success. The reminder shows on the roll as before.
- Situational skill bonuses such as *"+2 when demoralizing"* or *"+1 to decipher a scroll"*: PF1 already shows them on the roll, and whether the situation applies is the player's call.
