# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed
- **Pathfinder 1e Content is now required.** The spell buffs the module applies (*Resistance*, *Virtue*, *Guidance* and many more) come from its buff compendium; without it those spells gave an empty buff.

### New
- **Cantrips.** *Light* makes the target's token (or the caster's, with nothing targeted) shed normal light to 20 feet and dim light to 40; casting it again ends the earlier casting, and the token's own light returns when the spell ends. *Brand* deals its 1 point of damage on a failed save. *Haunted Fey Aspect* goes on the caster and ends when the caster takes damage. Utility cantrips (*Detect Magic*, *Mending*, *Prestidigitation* and others with nothing to apply to a creature) no longer get save or spell resistance rows on their card.
- ***Resist Energy*, *Death Knell* and *Lesser Restoration*.** *Resist Energy* asks its caster for the energy type when the card is posted; each creature it lands on resists that energy 10 (20 from caster level 7, 30 from 11), and the buff has a button to change it. *Death Knell* kills a creature at −1 hit points or fewer that fails its Will save, and gives the caster Pathfinder 1e Content's *Death Knell* buff (+2 Strength) with +1 caster level and 1d8 temporary hit points for 10 minutes per Hit Die of the creature; the temporary hit points left go when it ends. *Lesser Restoration* ends fatigue, improves exhausted to fatigued, and heals 1d4 damage to an ability score or ends a spell's penalty to one: the only option is applied at once, and with several the caster picks one on a card.
- **Core Rulebook 1st-level spells, first pass.** *Disguise Self* gives the caster +10 on Disguise. *Endure Elements*, *Feather Fall*, *Hide from Animals*, *Hide from Undead*, *Undetectable Alignment*, *Comprehend Languages* and *Speak with Animals* put a buff on their subjects for the duration with a reminder of what they do; *Pass Without Trace* uses Pathfinder 1e Content's buff. Utility spells (the detect spells, *Alarm*, *Identify*, *Silent Image*, *Ventriloquism*, *Magic Mouth*, the summons and others) no longer get rows for saves that are only made against objects or on interaction.
- **Situational bonuses in the roll window.** Bonuses PF1 prints only as reminders because they depend on a situation ("+1 on attack rolls when flanking", "+2 on attacks and damage when at negative hit points", "+2 if at or below half hit points with no conscious ally within 30 feet") now apply when the situation holds: the attack window's Flanking and Charge, raging, your hit points, allies around your token, the target's condition. Ones the module can't check yet (attacks of opportunity, a specific combat maneuver, "to jump after a running start") are listed in the window to tick. Features the module already automates on their own (Studied Target, ranged-combat feats, rogue talents, auras, some traits) aren't listed again. Feats and traits whose bonus PF1 states only in their description now get it too, from a table read from the text and reviewed by hand (about 60 feats and traits with attack, damage and combat maneuver bonuses, such as *Night Stalker* against flat-footed foes, *Orc Rampage* against shaken ones, *Ancient Tradition* against magical beasts; and about 60 with save bonuses against kinds of effects, such as *Pure Faith*'s +4 against poison, *Deny the Reaper* against death effects, *Scapegoat* against curses; and about 50 with skill bonuses, such as *Fey Friend* against fey or *Maze Expert* to avoid becoming lost).
- **More lasting areas.** *Grease* (a Reflex save or a fall at the casting; moving in it takes DC 10 Acrobatics, failing stops the creature and asks for a Reflex save, failing by 5 or more is a fall) and *Sleet Storm* (the same check, without the save); *Spike Growth* (1d4 piercing per 5 feet moved through it, measured along the move, then a Reflex save or half land speed for 24 hours); *Create Pit* (creatures in it at the casting save or fall in, taking 1d6 per 10 feet of depth; one walking in falls; one ending its turn next to it saves at +2). Fogs now give concealment through the miss chances: *Fog Cloud*, *Obscuring Mist*, *Solid Fog*, *Stinking Cloud* and *Cloudkill* give 20% within 5 feet and 50% beyond, when either creature is in the fog or the fog lies between them; *Sleet Storm* gives 50%.
- **Lasting areas.** *Entangle*, *Stinking Cloud*, *Cloudkill*, *Black Tentacles*, *Web*, *Solid Fog* and *Silence* keep working on the creatures in their area for the spell's duration. Creatures inside at the casting and those moving in later make the spell's save (or the tentacles' grapple check, or a check to move into a web), with a card and an Undo; on the caster's turn, those who saved and stayed in *Entangle* or *Stinking Cloud* save again, *Cloudkill* deals its Constitution damage and *Black Tentacles* rolls once for everyone inside. *Solid Fog*'s −2 and *Silence* apply while inside; entangled, grappled and nauseated end on leaving (*Stinking Cloud*'s nausea lasts 1d4+1 rounds more). When the duration ends the template is removed and everyone is released.

### Fixed
- **Spells whose area PF1 gives only in text now place a template.** About 50 spells state their area only in their Area or Effect line, so PF1 offered no template: their area's creatures got no rows, and the lasting areas of *Web*, *Stinking Cloud*, *Cloudkill*, *Solid Fog*, *Fog Cloud* and *Obscuring Mist* never started. A fixed radius ("20-ft.-radius spread") now gives a circle, a fixed line a line, and the detect spells' "cone-shaped emanation" a cone as long as the spell's range (*Flare Burst*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Rainbow Pattern*, *Sunburst* and others). Areas that grow with caster level, are made of cubes or can be shaped (walls, rings) are still placed by hand. The spell items themselves are not changed.
- **A condition given again now gets its new duration.** PF1 keeps the old duration when a creature already has the condition, so a monster's second paralyzing hit, an affliction's nausea on a creature already nauseated, and the rogue talents' staggered, unconscious and flat-footed didn't refresh. Every feature now gives conditions the same way: a longer duration replaces a shorter one.
- **Typed damage goes through damage reduction and energy resistance.** *Black Tentacles* (bludgeoning), *Spike Growth* (piercing), a fall into *Create Pit*, and spell damage dealt on a hit (*Chill Touch*'s negative energy) were applied as plain damage. The card notes when DR reduced it, Undo gives back exactly what was taken, and *Spike Growth*'s Reflex save comes only after damage is actually taken.
- **The GM now sees every private message about a creature.** Private cards a player's own game posted (an affliction's save results, for example) went to the player only; a GM doesn't see a private message that isn't addressed to them. Every private message about a creature now goes to its owners and every GM.
- **A burning creature's Reflex save counts bonuses against fire**, as afflictions' saves count bonuses against poison and disease.
- **Lighter bookkeeping on every actor preparation and every tick of game time.** The typed-penalty correction now does its work only after a change turns out to be a penalty (it was recording positions before every typed change), and afflictions and lasting areas check synchronously whether anything is due before starting any work. A fifth performance review puts the module's share of a simulated session at 0.6%, now including afflictions, lasting areas and token movement.

## 0.24.0: poison, disease and curses; typed penalties

### New
- **Poisoned weapons, cures and treatment.** A poison's card has **Apply to a weapon…**: pick a weapon or ammunition; there's a 5% chance of poisoning yourself (none with Poison Use), and only injury and contact poisons go on a weapon, which then shows "Poisoned". The next hit the module can see delivers the dose to the creature hit, and a natural 1 exposes the wielder; either way the dose is used up. *Neutralize Poison*, *Remove Disease* and *Remove Curse* roll a caster level check against each poison, disease or curse on the target and end the ones they beat (damage already dealt stays). *Delay Poison* holds poisons off until it ends, including the first effect of a poison contracted meanwhile; *Pernicious Poison* gives −4 on saves against poison and on attempts to cure it, and poisons last 2 more saves. **Treat (Heal)…** on an affliction's buff picks a healer, whose Heal check is rolled with every save after that (+4 on the save when it beats the DC) until **Stop treating**. Resting no longer heals the ability damage and hit points an affliction dealt while it lasts; a note says why.
- **Poison, disease and curses are tracked.** A contracted affliction becomes a buff on the creature that keeps its schedule. It's contracted from a monster's "plus poison" or "plus disease" (which used to roll only the first save) or from the **Expose targets** button on the card of any poison, disease or ability that describes one. Nothing happens until the onset has passed; without an onset the failed first save applies the effect at once. Periodic saves come due at the start of the creature's turn for "/round" afflictions in combat, and when game time passes the due time for longer ones: a player's character gets a private card with a button to roll, while creatures no player owns roll on their own, and a "/round" poison outside combat offers **Resolve all**. Each failed save applies its effect automatically, with an Undo: ability damage and drain, conditions (for their duration, or while the affliction lasts) and hit point damage, with initial and secondary effects. It ends after the cure's number of saves in a row, or when its duration runs out; another dose of an active poison raises the DC by 2 and adds half its duration. Undead and constructs are immune to poison and disease, and save bonuses against poison, disease and curses apply.

### Fixed
- **Typed penalties stack as the rules say.** PF1 keeps only the highest change of each type, so a typed penalty (a −1 morale penalty) disappeared next to a bonus of the same type, and of two such penalties the milder one counted; on the character sheet the result could even depend on the order the buffs were added. Now a penalty applies alongside bonuses of its type, and of two penalties of one type the worst applies, on sheet totals (saves, skills, AC, ability scores) and on attack and damage rolls, where the penalty shows as its own line. Untyped penalties are unchanged. A world setting, *Typed penalties stack as the rules say*, turns this off to keep PF1's own math.

## 0.23.0: weapon spells, amulet of mighty fists, armor abilities

### New
- **Amulet of mighty fists.** Its enhancement bonus now also applies to unarmed strikes (PF1 gave it to natural weapons only), and counts against damage reduction as a weapon's would: magic at +1, cold iron and silver at +3, adamantine at +4, alignments at +5 (Paizo FAQ). The amulet gets the Magic Qualities section: its abilities (flaming, holy, bane and the others the module automates) apply to the wearer's unarmed strikes and natural weapons; abilities that need piercing, slashing or ranged weapons (keen, vorpal, seeking) aren't offered, no +1 is needed first, and the total can't go above +5. It's named from its abilities ("+1 Flaming Amulet of Mighty Fists") and priced at 4,000 gp × the total bonus squared. A speed amulet gives one extra attack a round, not one per natural weapon.
- **Armor and shield abilities.** While worn: shadow and slick add their +5, +10 or +15 competence bonus to Stealth or Escape Artist (on the character sheet's totals); energy resistance resists 10, 20 or 30 of the energy chosen in the Magic Qualities section; spell resistance (13 to 19) applies unless the wearer's own is higher; invulnerability gives DR 5/magic. They're also read from the armor's name ("+1 Greater Shadow Leather Armor", "Improved Fire Resistance Breastplate"). Resistances and DR from armor don't stack with others of their kind: the best applies.
- **Weapon spells act on one weapon.** *Magic weapon*, *greater magic weapon*, *keen edge*, *align weapon* and *bless weapon* go on the target's only fitting weapon, or the first of several with a private message to choose another (also on the buff's sheet); *align weapon* asks for its alignment. *Magic weapon*'s enhancement bonus now counts for that weapon only, and not with the weapon's own (the higher applies). The weapon counts as magic against damage reduction (greater magic weapon's bonus bypasses nothing else); *keen edge* makes a piercing or slashing weapon keen; *align weapon* adds its alignment against DR, except on a weapon that already has one; *bless weapon* makes the weapon good-aligned (suppressing any other alignment), magic against evil creatures' DR, and its critical threats against an evil foe are marked confirmed on the card, unless the weapon is already keen or vorpal. What a spell adds shows in the weapon's Magic Qualities section ("For a time") and counts toward the +10 limit, but never changes the weapon's name or price. *Greater magic weapon*, *keen edge* and *align weapon* are now applied from the spell's card.

### Changed
- **Cleaner attack cards.** A buff turned off in the roll window now simply leaves PF1's own line ("+1[Haste]" is gone) instead of staying with a correction beside it ("−1[Buffs: Haste off]"); a bonus switched on shows as its own line. Conditions turned off, and saves and skill checks, still use the labeled correction.
- **Penalties from another creature's spell are locked** in roll windows like conditions, so only the GM can turn them off for a roll (for example *Bane* cast by an enemy through the spell rows).
- **Locked rows stand out** in roll windows: a solid amber bar and bold text, unlike the faded, dashed Not Applied rows.

### Fixed
- **The Magic Qualities section appears with the rest of the item sheet** instead of a moment later, and costs about 1.5 ms per sheet: it no longer loads the compendium item each time to read its price. A fourth performance review puts the module's share of a simulated session at 0.5%, now counting all of its 175 hooks and its method wrappers.

## 0.22.0: weapon qualities, magic arms and materials, monster attacks

### New
- **Speed, furious and defending weapons.** A speed weapon ticks PF1's Haste extra attack on a full attack (one extra attack at full base attack bonus, never a second one with *haste*), also when the attack window is skipped. A furious weapon's enhancement bonus is +2 better while its wielder rages (rage, bloodrage, inspired rage or the *rage* spell), and with raging climber, raging swimmer or raging leaper the wielder adds the weapon's enhancement bonus to that skill while wielding it. A defending weapon's wielder gets a private message at the start of each of their turns in combat with buttons to move some of the weapon's enhancement bonus to AC; last turn's choice carries over, so nothing needs clicking unless it changes. The AC is a buff until their next turn, the weapon's attacks lose the same amount, and the buff ends with the combat.
- **Incorporeal creatures take damage as the rules say.** PF1 marks them with its Incorporeal condition but applied full damage from everything. Damage applied from a card to an incorporeal creature is now 0 from nonmagical attacks, and half (minimum 1) from magic weapons, spells and supernatural or spell-like abilities of corporeal creatures; ghost touch weapons, force effects (*magic missile*), channel energy and other incorporeal creatures deal full damage. A whispered note says why.
- **Weapon qualities that act on a hit.** Wounding's bleed (1 per hit, adding up with each hit; creatures immune to critical hits are immune), disruption (an undead hit makes a DC 14 Will save or is destroyed) and thundering's deafness (a DC 14 Fortitude save on a confirmed critical hit) are applied as soon as the module can tell the attack hit, with an Undo, or by an Apply button, like monster attack riders. Vorpal is the GM's call: on a natural 20 that's confirmed, the GM gets a message and a **Sever** button on the card. A brilliant energy weapon adds the target's armor and shield bonuses to its attack roll (they don't count against it) and deals no damage to undead and constructs. A seeking ranged weapon ignores the target's miss chances. Ghost touch is now read from the weapon's qualities for miss chances and sneak attack.
- **Magic arms and materials.** Weapons, armor and shields get a **Magic Qualities** section on their Details tab, much like the rune slots in the PF2e system. Abilities are added from the full list in Pathfinder 1e Content (with a search box and a link to each one's text), and the section checks the rules: an ability needs at least a +1 enhancement bonus, the total can't go above +10, and some abilities fit only some weapons (keen only on piercing or slashing melee weapons). It shows the price worked out from the parts (masterwork, special material, the bonus and abilities priced in gp, cold iron's +2,000 gp for enchanting), and **Update price…** sets it, asking for the plain item's price. While an item keeps its stock name, it's named from its parts ("+1 Flaming Cold Iron Longsword"); a custom name is kept, and a **specific magic item** (Frost Brand, Holy Avenger) keeps its name and price. Special materials now do what their rules say: hardness and hit points (adamantine: hardness 20 and a third more hit points), weight, masterwork, mithral and darkwood's armor check penalty, maximum Dexterity and spell failure, mithral armor counting as one category lighter for speed and limitations (not proficiency), adamantine armor's DR 1/—, 2/— or 3/—, and alchemical silver's −1 on damage with slashing and piercing weapons. Players don't see an unidentified item's abilities. Fortification chosen on armor counts, whatever the armor is called.
- **Weapon qualities.** A weapon's magic qualities are read from its Magic Qualities section, or from its name ("+1 Flaming Burst Longsword", "+1 Undead Bane Longsword") when nothing is chosen there. Their damage is added in the roll window, where each can be unticked: flaming, frost, shock and corrosive add 1d6 of their energy on a hit; the burst qualities and thundering add their dice on a critical hit (1d10 at ×2, 2d10 at ×3, 3d10 at ×4; d8s of sonic for thundering), even while the base quality is off; holy, unholy, axiomatic and anarchic add 2d6 against creatures of the opposed alignment and count as that alignment against damage reduction; bane adds +2 on attack and damage rolls and 2d6 against its designated foe; vicious adds 2d6 and deals 1d6 to the wielder on each hit (applied once the module can tell the attack hit, with an Undo); merciful adds 1d6 and makes the attack's damage nonlethal. Keen doubles the threat range of piercing and slashing melee weapons, unless something already expanded it. The extra dice apply to each arrow of Manyshot. When the action already has the die typed into its damage, it isn't added twice.
- **Burn, rend and the monster's grapple.** A monster's "plus burn" deals its fire damage on a melee hit and rolls the target's Reflex save; on a failure the target catches fire for 1d4 rounds, taking the damage at the start of each of its turns, with buttons to put it out (a full-round Reflex save, or dropping and rolling for +4). Hitting a creature that burns with a natural weapon or unarmed strike burns the attacker. Rend is offered on the card once the monster has hit the same target with the attacks its entry names that round, once a round. After a successful grab, the GM gets a card at the start of each of the monster's turns to maintain the grapple (dealing the grab attack's damage and constrict), rake, swallow whole or let go; a swallowed creature takes its damage each round until it's out. Blood drain is applied at the end of the monster's turn while it's attached or grappling. A stirge's touch attack now attaches.
- **Monster attack riders.** A monster's attack that hits with "plus grab", "plus trip", "plus poison", "plus paralysis", "plus bleed", "plus attach" or "plus energy drain" (or the same as a linked ability) shows them under the attack. Paralysis, poison, disease, bleed, attach and energy drain happen on every hit, so they're applied as soon as the module can tell the attack hit, with an Undo button: the target's save is rolled against the DC from the creature's entry, paralysis lasts the duration given there, energy drain gives twice the negative levels on a confirmed critical and 5 temporary hit points per level to the drainer, and poison and disease roll their first save and say what to apply. Grab, trip, pull and push are the monster's choice: a button rolls its combat maneuver check against the target's CMD (grab with its +4, or a hold at −20 that doesn't grapple the monster), applies grappled or prone on a success, and respects the size limits; constrict deals its damage after a successful grab. When the module can tell an attack missed, its riders say so and offer **Apply anyway** instead of applying; once the target is dead, a full attack's later hits apply nothing more to it.
- **Critical feats on the card.** Under each attack that threatens a critical hit, the card offers the attacker's critical feats as buttons. Once the critical is confirmed, the attacker picks one (two with Critical Mastery) and it's applied to the creature hit, with its Fortitude save (DC 10 + base attack bonus) rolled: Staggering, Stunning, Sickening, Tiring, Exhausting, Blinding, Deafening, Bleeding, Censoring, Crippling and Flaying Critical, and Destroy Identity. Repeated hits add to Staggering, Stunning and Sickening Critical's duration, Bleeding Critical's bleed stacks, and Flaying Critical's penalty adds up until the encounter ends. Undead and constructs ignore the Fortitude effects. Critical feats the module can't resolve are offered too and count toward the limit. PF1's footnotes for these feats are hidden so nothing is applied twice.

## 0.21.0: auras, damage reduction, creatures

### New

**Auras**
- **Auras affect the creatures around them**, added and removed as tokens move: paladin Aura of Courage, Resolve and Righteousness; antipaladin Aura of Cowardice, Despair and Depravity; Foretell; the enchanter's Aura of Despair; cleric Aura of Heroism, Protection (domain and warpriest blessing) and Menace. Each creature inside gets a buff with the aura's bonuses, penalties, save notes or energy resistance; the same aura from two creatures counts once.
- **Auras used in rounds per day** start from the feature's Use button, ask how many rounds to spend (and, for Foretell, whether to aid or hinder), and give back the unused rounds when the aura's buff is turned off. Auras work on the scene in play: the active scene, or one a connected user is viewing.

**Damage reduction, resistances and immunities**
- **Features and templates give their DR, resistances and immunities**: barbarian and bloodrager Damage Reduction, Armor Mastery, monk Diamond Body, Purity of Body and Perfect Self, paladin Divine Health and aura defenses, antipaladin DR, Venom Immunity, Poison Immunity, sorcerer bloodline resistances and 20th-level powers, and the Celestial, Fiendish, Entropic, Resolute, Shadow Creature, Half-Celestial, Half-Fiend, Vampire, Lich, Skeleton and Zombie templates, scaling with level or Hit Dice. Powers with "your energy type" ask for it once; the Energy Resistance rage power works while raging, Armor Mastery in armor, and Death's Gift's DR against nonlethal damage only.
- **Only the best damage reduction counts**, as the rules say: PF1's dialog subtracted every DR that applied (DR 5/— and DR 10/magic stopped 15 from a mundane sword).
- **DR applies to damage applied from the card**, with the weapon's material, alignment and enhancement, as in PF1's dialog. The setting is renamed "Apply resistance and DR on quick damage".

**Creatures**
- **Bonuses against kinds of creatures** written as context notes ("+1 Racial vs Humanoids (Orc, Goblinoid)", "+4 Dodge vs Giants", "+1 vs evil creatures") apply on their own: attack rolls and weapon damage against the targets, AC and CMD against the attacker, skill and ability checks, and saves against effects from that kind of creature. Items that say to choose the creatures (variant Hatred, Defensive Training, Lesser) ask for the choice.
- **Monster templates change a creature's type**: a half-fiend goblin is an outsider, not also a humanoid. When two templates change the type, the GM chooses which counts.
- **Fast healing and regeneration** heal the creature at the start of each of its turns in combat; fire, acid or another listed damage type stops regeneration for its next turn. Infernal Healing and Celestial Healing put their buff on the target.

**Saves**
- **Saves against abilities that aren't spells**: class abilities, hexes, monster and template abilities, racial traits and alchemical items with a save get the spell rows, with the condition and duration read from their text. Delivered by an attack, only the creatures it hit are affected; when the effect can't be read, the row still rolls the save and says to apply it by hand.

### Fixed
- **A player applying damage to a creature they don't control** no longer gets a "resists … Took …" line: PF1 refuses the damage, and the module now stays quiet too.
- **Lullaby and Hypnotism affect only living creatures**, as their areas say; undead and constructs are unaffected.
- **Nauseating Dart rolls its ranged attack against full AC** ("as though you had fired a dart from a blowgun") and deals 1d2 piercing on a hit; the Fortitude save against nausea follows only a hit.
- **Faerie fire removes blur, displacement and invisibility miss chances** against the outlined creature. PF1's Concealed condition is still rolled, since it doesn't say whether it comes from darkness.
- **Blindsight ignores concealment within its range**, and **Improved Precise Shot ignores miss chances below total concealment** on ranged attacks (displacement and invisibility still count). PF1's Blindsight item records its range as blindsense; the range is read from the item as well.
- **All-around vision stops flanking from allowing sneak attack.**
- **Channel resistance applies**: "+4 to resist channel energy" is added to saves against channel energy and effects that rely on it.
- **Negative energy affinity**: dhampirs and other living creatures with it are healed by negative energy and harmed by positive energy (channel energy, cure and inflict spells) while staying living creatures.
- **A cancelled channel** (or one without a token) gives the use back and removes PF1's card, which showed the uses left before the refund.
- **Monster templates change a creature's type** instead of adding to it: a half-fiend goblin is an outsider (native), not also a humanoid, and a human zombie is undead without the human subtype. Each template's change follows its rules text, including the subtypes it keeps or drops; the template's note lists dropped subtypes, and adding one to the template's Creature Subtypes keeps it. When two templates change the type, a window asks which one sets it.
- **Hand-typed subtypes match** regardless of capitals, spaces and hyphens, and PF1's misspelled "Reptillian" subtype (kobolds, lizardfolk) counts as reptilian.
- **A sneak attack talent that isn't once a day no longer uses up a charge** when it was already the talent chosen last time.
- **A longer condition now replaces a shorter one timed in rounds.** A condition applied by PF1 or another module in rounds was treated as permanent, so the spell's longer duration was dropped.
- **Players see their own hidden token's row** on a spell card and can roll its save; other players still don't see it.
- **Scrolls, wands and potions of spells with spell resistance and no save** now get their rows and spell resistance check.
- **A linked character's save counts when rolled from another token or scene** than the one that was targeted.
- **No more duplicate buffs with Nevela's Automation Suite** when Nevela applies a buff chosen for the action, or a buff whose name only partly matches the spell's ("Bless (Mythic)").
- **Scrolls, wands and potions find their spell in any compendium** (pf-content, homebrew) and whatever the capitalization of the item's name, so spell resistance and immunities apply.
- **Cure and inflict wands, scrolls and potions are reversed on undead** like the spells, and an inflict spell's row on an undead creature says "healing".

## 0.20.0: spells on their targets, sneak attack, ranged combat

### New

**Spells**
- **Spells on their targets.** A spell's chat card lists every creature it affects, one row each. Spell resistance is rolled for you (only the GM sees the SR value), touch and ray spells are checked against touch AC, each creature's save is rolled from its row (or with PF1's save button), and the result is applied as soon as it's known: conditions with their duration, the spell's buff at the caster's level, or a penalty. Harmless spells on allies need no save. Immunities (mind-affecting, Hit Dice limits, creature types, sightless creatures, condition immunities) are found when the spell lands, never shown beforehand. A hidden token's row is shown to the GM only.
- **Over 120 cantrips and 1st-level spells apply their effects**, from the Core Rulebook, Advanced Player's Guide, Ultimate Magic, Ultimate Combat and other books: Daze, Sleep, Color Spray (by Hit Dice), Cause Fear, Ray of Enfeeblement, Bless, Bane, Shield of Faith, Mage Armor, Enlarge Person and more. Fear stacks (shaken twice becomes frightened). Scrolls, wands and potions work like the spell.
- **Touch spells roll their touch attack** when PF1's data has none (Touch of Fatigue, Chill Touch, Touch of Blindness); allies accept a touch without a roll. Personal spells go on the caster.
- **Saves to end an effect**: Hideous Laughter offers its new save on the creature's next turn; Mudball, Shadow Trap and Unlock Flesh every turn until one succeeds. The save window adds Hypnotism's and Hideous Laughter's situational modifiers.
- **Fixes to PF1's spell data**: Magic Missile fires one missile per two caster levels beyond 1st (up to five) and hits automatically; cure spells harm undead and inflict spells heal them; Disrupt Undead only affects undead; Chill Touch deals its damage and Strength damage.

**Sneak attack**
- **Sneak attack is added automatically** when the target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC or, in melee, flanked. Dice from every Sneak Attack feature stack, and they're typed as precision damage, so a creature immune to precision damage takes none when the damage is applied (not revealed before the attack). Range, concealment, Uncanny Dodge and Improved Uncanny Dodge are respected.
- **Feats, items, traits and spells** that change sneak attack: Accomplished Sneak Attacker, Sense Vitals, Sneaking Critical, Savage Critical, Sniper Goggles, Shadow Strike, the Headband of Ninjitsu and many more.
- **Sneak attack talents**: a **With:** dropdown chooses a talent that adds an effect or replaces the dice (Bleeding Attack, Crippling Strike, Knock-Out Blow, Powerful Sneak and others). The card shows the effect and its save DC, and an **Apply** button rolls the save and applies it.
- **Bleed** is tracked with its amount: the creature takes it at the start of each of its turns until healed or a DC 15 Heal check stops it.
- **Fortification** is rolled once per critical threat or sneak attack against a creature that has it, and a negation drops the critical damage and sneak attack dice. One-off negations (Bracelet of Second Chances, Jingasa of the Fortunate Soldier, Fortified) get a button on the card.

**Ranged combat**
- **Range**: Point-Blank Shot is ticked within 30 ft and left off beyond. Feats, traits and items that change range increments or penalties (Far Shot, Distance Thrower, Longshot and others) feed Roll Bonuses' range penalty.
- **Defenses**: the card shows a target's AC against the ranged attack when something changes it (prone, Bullet Shield, Low Profile and others) to the GM and the target's owner; Deflect Arrows and similar abilities get a **Deflect** button; damage reduction against ranged weapons applies from the card.
- **Special shots and firearms**: firearms against touch AC at close range, Aspect of the Falcon, Toxophilite, Halfling Slinger, Arrow-Splitter, Large Target, Coordinated Shot, and Bullseye Shot, Pinpoint Targeting and Opening Volley to tick.
- **Loaded weapons (optional, off by default)**: crossbows, slings and firearms track their loaded shots and must be reloaded with a **Reload** button; reload times follow the weapon and feats such as Rapid Reload.
- **Shooting while threatened**: the card notes that a ranged attack next to an enemy provokes attacks of opportunity, unless an ability prevents it.

### Fixed
- **Monsters with racial Hit Dice and no race now have a creature type.** PF1's bestiary builds many monsters this way (a Human Skeleton has "Undead" class levels), and PF1 doesn't read the type from the class. Channel energy now treats such skeletons and zombies as undead.
- **Nevela's Automation Suite and PF1e Revamp no longer both apply a buff.** Nevela applies spell buffs by default; PF1e Revamp now leaves a buff to Nevela when Nevela will apply it, and applies it itself otherwise.
- **Several tokens of the same creature** (unlinked tokens of one actor) no longer share a roll window's bonuses or one-roll buffs such as Guidance.
- **Stepping the combat tracker back and forward again** no longer replaces a wizard's stored Prescience roll.
- **Natural 1s and 20s** count on channel energy's saves.
- **Bonuses of the same type on different rolls both count** in the roll window: a trait bonus on attack rolls no longer hides a trait bonus on damage rolls.
- **Immunity to precision or nonlethal damage** now blocks damage that has another type as well ("piercing, precision"), in PF1's Apply Damage dialog and from a chat card.
- **Ticking a checkbox in the attack window** (such as Flanking) keeps the choices already made in the roll's buff list.

## 0.19.0: buffs on a roll, clearer windows, and rules fixes

### New
- **Buffs on a roll.** Attack, save and skill windows show what PF1's buffs and conditions add to the roll. A few show as chips you can untick; more than that (4 by default, a per-player setting) show as totals with a **Manage…** window that groups them by attack, damage or both, combines ability-score buffs, and lists what's **Not Applied**: bonuses beaten by a higher one of the same type, and situational ones that don't apply right now. Changes apply to that roll only and show on the chat card as one labeled part. Conditions can only be turned off by the GM. Ability checks get the same line. Alignment wards and save bonuses against effects ("+2 vs fear") are listed too: when the save isn't rolled from a matching effect's card they show as Not Applied with the reason, and can be ticked by hand.
- **Variant channeling** (optional rule from *Ultimate Magic*, off by default under Settings). New clerics choose standard channel energy or one of 45 portfolio variants in the level-up window; existing channelers choose once from Channel Energy's note, and the choice locks (the GM can reset it). Healing with positive energy or harming with negative energy is halved per the rules (on PF1's own card, so chat shows one number, and never below 1). The choice appears on the sheet as a "Variant Channeling (portfolio)" class feature with its rules text. **Luck** is fully automated: healed creatures get a one-roll bonus, and the negative-energy version applies a d20 penalty unless the save succeeds.
- **Searchable dropdowns everywhere.** Any list the module adds with more than 10 options has a search box: bloodlines, domains, arcane schools, the new channeling choice, and long trait choices (like Gifted Adept's spell list).

- **One-roll bonuses are the player's choice.** Guidance, True Strike and the Luck channel bonus show as a ticked box in every roll window (attack, save, skill, ability check); untick it to roll without it and keep the bonus for later.

### Changed
- **Save cards mark reminder notes the module already applied.** A note such as Bravery's "+2 vs fear effects" is ticked and faded, with "already added to this roll" on hover, when that bonus is in the roll, so it isn't added twice by hand. Notes for bonuses that didn't apply stay as normal reminders.
- **Every window the module opens names the creature it's for** ("Choose a slayer talent (level 4) – Kestrel", "Channel Positive Energy – Sister Aurel"), like PF1's own roll windows.
- **Controls that can't be used say why.** The talent/feat picker's Choose button stays off until you pick something ("Pick one first"); greyed-out archetype rows, channel-energy targets, racial ability choices and the off-hand weapon list explain themselves on hover.
- **Saving throws from a card warn when they'd roll for the wrong creature.** PF1's save button rolls for whatever token is selected; if that isn't one of the card's targets, you're told who it's rolling for (the roll still goes ahead).
- Studied Target, Quarry and the Weapon Finesse shield penalty appear in the roll window (the shield penalty only the GM can turn off). Studied Target and Quarry and can be left out of a roll. When no studied creature or quarry is targeted, they're listed as Not Applied and can be ticked by hand (for theater-of-mind play).
- **Halved damage and healing never go below 1.** PF1's ½ buttons (and any ratio in its Apply Damage dialog) used to turn 1 point into nothing; a save for half on channel energy too. Per the core rule, damage and hit points have a minimum of 1.
- Channel energy no longer posts its own summary message; PF1's card shows the amount and saves have their own cards. The request to the GM is whispered and removed once applied.

### Fixed
- **Installs and updates now get the right version.** The manifest's download link still pointed at the 0.17.0 package, so installing or updating to 0.18.0 delivered 0.17.0. The release process now refuses a release whose download link doesn't match it.
- **Halved damage against resistance with Nevela's Automation Suite.** Its chat-card damage buttons set the ½ ratio without recomputing PF1's reductions, so immunity, DR and energy resistance were worked out on the unhalved damage and then halved again: 6 slashing + 4 fire, halved, against fire resistance 10 dealt 1 instead of 3. Such targets are now recomputed with PF1's own method; PF1's normal path is unaffected.
- **Energy resistance on halved damage.** With the ½ button, damage is now halved before resistance is subtracted (the common reading, as the rules don't specify): 20 fire, saved for half, against fire resistance 10 is fully resisted instead of dealing 5. The private note now shows the damage actually taken. Damage of several types is split so each type gets its proportional share of the halved total (an immunity to one type no longer cancels the others); when PF1 doesn't say how much of each type there was, the amounts are read from the attack card, or the damage is left for the GM rather than guessed. Later hooks still see the ½ ratio.
- **Conditional save bonuses and penalties are no longer applied automatically.** Notes such as "+1 vs charm and compulsion effects if a partner is within 60 feet" (Tight Bonds), "−2 vs fear effects whenever the object of your attachment is threatened" (Attached) or "vs effects created by outsiders" were applied to every matching save. They now appear under Not Applied with their condition, to be ticked when it applies. Their "only vs …" text is also tidier.
- Double Slice uses the off-hand weapon's damage ability, and the Strength modifier after any buff left out of the roll in the roll window.
- Character trait choices: when a choice has nothing available yet, Save is greyed out and says why, instead of doing nothing.
- Racial trait review: a search box filters long trait lists (humans have dozens of alternate traits).
- Studied Target's indicator buffs escape creature names in their descriptions.
- **One-roll bonuses follow the stacking rules.** Guidance (+1 competence) adds nothing beside a higher competence bonus such as Inspire Courage, so it's no longer offered or used up on that roll; it waits for one where it helps. True Strike and the Luck channel bonus are capped the same way.
- Luck variant channeling: a creature harmed by two channels in a row had two penalties; the second channel now refreshes the first.
- Saves: a bonus vs fear beaten by a higher bonus of the same type (Bless's +1 morale beside Greater Heroism) said "only vs fear" even against a fear effect; it now says a higher morale bonus already applies.
- Racial trait review: a standard trait's ability choice is greyed out when the trait is unticked, including when an alternate unticks it for you.
- A feature granted twice at different levels by an archetype or subclass (the same item at, say, 1st and 5th) could have both copies recorded at one level.
- The one-time repair that ties older bloodlines, domains and schools to their class ran on every world load; it now runs once per world.
- **Quarry and True Strike no longer stack.** Both are insight bonuses, so only the higher counts: an attack with True Strike gets +20, not +22 (or +24 with Improved Quarry); Quarry still applies to the rest of a full attack. Quarry also defers to any other insight bonus on attacks the slayer already has.
- Bloodlines, domains and schools are now tied to their class, so their formulas scale: the Protection domain's resistance bonus stayed +1 instead of +1 per 5 levels (+3 at 10th). Existing characters are repaired when the GM next loads the world.
- Foretell (Foresight subschool) lasts rounds per day equal to wizard level; PF1's data used the Int modifier.
- The arcane school picker for a new wizard only listed subschools; the nine regular schools are back.
- Opposition schools no longer offer "Miscellaneous", which isn't a school.

## 0.18.0: slayer talents, ranger styles, read-before-you-choose, and a round of fixes

### New
- **Slayer talent picker.** It appears in PF1's level-up window at every even slayer level, and on the Slayer Talents feature for catching up.
  - It has a search box, and marks each talent with requirements met / not met / check manually. Advanced talents show from 10th level.
  - Follow-up choices are asked right away:
    - **Ranger Combat Style**: the style, then a feat with prerequisites ignored; the 6th- and 10th-level lists open when it's taken again.
    - **Rogue Talent**, plus the advanced and catfolk variants. Shared rogue talents are only offered through it.
    - **Combat Trick** and **Feat**: feat picker with prerequisite marks, plus a "Browse all feats" button that opens PF1's Feat Browser.
    - Grit, Toxin Training, Focusing Attack and Terrain Mastery ask their own question.
  - Talents that grant a specific feat add it. Deleting a talent removes the feats it brought.
- **Read before you choose.** Every option in the module's pickers has a 📖 icon that opens the full entry: talents, feats, combat styles, racial traits, archetype features, bloodline/domain/school powers. Level-up dropdowns get one icon for the selected entry.
- **Ranger combat style feats** at 2nd/6th/10th/14th/18th level: choose the style once, then feats from the lists unlocked at your level, prerequisites ignored.

### Fixed
- From manual QA:
  - Racial trait review: **Not now** after "Review again…" no longer removes your existing traits and ability bonus. The old choice is undone only when a new one is applied, and the review opens with your current choices ticked.
  - Two alternate traits that replace the same trait (Heart of the Mountains and Heart of the Streets) can't both be taken; ticking one unticks the other, and unticking an alternate gives back what it replaced.
  - Dual Talent and other "two different ability scores" picks start on different abilities and can't be set to the same one (it gave +4 to one score).
  - Channel Energy's use dialog no longer starts with "Measure Template" ticked.
- Found by sweeping every compendium entry through the module's parsers:
  - Feat prerequisites: skill ranks, races, size, base saves, Hit Dice, "ability to cast … spells", class features and "X or Y" are now checked. Things that aren't feats are no longer marked ✗ "feat not found" (1,302 wrong marks across 3,564 feats). Feat names containing "and" (Craft Magic Arms and Armor) are no longer split in two.
  - Rogue and Ninja Advanced Talents now offers Evasion (Trick) and, as the rules allow, any rogue advanced talent. The talent lists are read from the entries' links instead of matching names.
  - Underhanded and Underwater combat styles were missing their first feat list.
  - Racial trait review: alternates that replace several traits separated by semicolons now replace all of them. "Language" matches "Languages". Replaced items that aren't trait items ("Base Statistics", or a trait pf-content lacks) are marked "adjust by hand".
  - Bloodline/domain/school review: power names no longer pick up headings ("Solitude Domain Powers Resistant Touch"); powers found under a slightly different name aren't reported missing; Fey Bloodline's Woodland Stride arrives at 3rd level, not 1st.

  - Shield, armor, simple and martial weapon proficiency now count however you got them, not only as a feat (a slayer's class shield proficiency qualifies for Shield Focus).
  - "8th-level fighter" checks fighter levels, not character level.
  - "Light Armor Proficiency, Medium Armor Proficiency" is read correctly.
  - Requirements that depend on your choice ("Weapon Focus with selected weapon") say "check manually" instead of ✗.

- Racial trait review: Adoptive Parentage (Weapon Focus) and 14 Changeling and Dhampir heritage traits showed as standard traits instead of alternates, because their "Replaced Trait(s)" label is formatted differently in pf-content.

## 0.17.0: first public test release

The first release for testing at a real table. Built and tested on Foundry VTT v13 (build 351) with the Pathfinder 1e system 11.11.

### What's included

**Character options**
- Archetype application with real mechanical changes: altered features are renamed "Feature (Archetype)" and changed, restatements are merged instead of duplicated, and removal restores everything. Mechanics are written out for Battle Scion, Blossoming Light and Chronomancer.
- Bloodline, domain, arcane school and subschool powers granted at the right levels. Specialist wizards pick opposition schools, whose spells cost two slots.
- Racial trait review with standard and alternate traits, the "+2 to one ability of your choice" picker, real energy resistance from racial traits, and no double-counted race bonuses.
- Energy resistances to the same energy never stack (highest only).
- Character traits: Criminal, Dilettante Artist, Magical Knack (with its Hit Dice cap), Gifted Adept, Swordlord's Page, Student of Philosophy ("with Int" second total).

**Combat**
- Two-weapon fighting (penalties, off-hand attacks, Improved/Greater TWF, Double Slice, Two-Weapon Defense, Two-Weapon Rend).
- Shield bashes: fixes PF1's −4 penalty; Shield-Trained; Weapon Finesse with light bashes.
- Miss chances rolled on attack cards (Blur, Displacement, Blink, Entropic Shield and more), with true seeing, see invisibility and Blind-Fight handled.
- Alignment wards: AC and save bonuses against the opposed alignment only.
- Save bonuses against effects (fear, poison, enchantment, charm, mind-affecting, death, spells...) from racial traits, character traits and buffs, following PF1's stacking rules. A racial bonus stated on both the race and one of its traits counts once.
- Next-roll effects: True Strike, Guidance, Bit of Luck, Prescience.
- Energy resistance and immunity on quick-applied damage.

**Class features**
- Cleric: Channel Energy (burst targeting, automatic healing and Will saves, Selective Channeling, Blossoming Light), Spontaneous Casting, Bit of Luck, Resistant Touch.
- Skald: Raging Song with target checklist, per-round costs and deafness failure.
- Slayer: Studied Target, Quarry, Improved Quarry.
- Sorcerer: bloodline powers (Solar fully supported), bloodline bonus-feat picker.
- Wizard: Chronomancer Temporal Pool (Forewarned, Rewind), Foresight subschool, opposition schools.

### Testing
- 113 automated tests (Quench), all passing.
- GM-relay features played through as a non-GM player.
- Two performance reviews: the module's share of a simulated session is 0.20%.

### Known limitations
- Save bonuses only apply when the save is rolled from the effect's chat card (PF1's save button).
- Blossoming Light's channel energy can't detect "worshipers of chaotic evil deities"; the GM applies those by hand.
- Oppressive Expectations and Dependent stay as reminder notes, since they trigger on a failed check and most checks in play have no DC entered.
- Archetypes other than Battle Scion, Blossoming Light and Chronomancer get structural changes and renamed features, but their bonuses aren't written out yet.
