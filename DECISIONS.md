# PF1e Revamp – Design decisions

Rulings made with the project lead, so later changes don't quietly undo them. Read the entries for a
feature before changing it. Newest first within each section. Format: **decision**: why (date).

## General UX
- **What players see about enemies**: random rolls (miss chances, fortification) are shown on cards to everyone, since they're chance outcomes; fixed enemy statistics (AC, damage reduction, immunities) are not: they show only to the GM and the creature's owner, or take effect without being announced (precision immunity) (2026-10-25).
- **Raging Song keeps the plain "no rounds left" message**, also when the daily rounds were never filled (a new feature before the first rest); no separate "rest first" wording (2026-10-24).
- **Every window the module opens names the creature it acts for** (`util.titleFor`: "Title – Name", PF1's style), and **every control that can't act says why** (tooltip or message), never silently doing nothing. PF1's card save button rolls for the selected token: warn (don't block) when that isn't a card target. From the PF2e study, where a save rolled for the wrong creature unnoticed (2026-10-23).
- **Any dropdown or list the module adds with more than 10 options gets a search box** (`util.addSearch` / `searchSelects`): long lists are painful to scroll (2026-10-23).
- **Every picker option has a 📖 icon that opens the full compendium entry**: players need to read an option before choosing it (2026-10-23).
- **Optional rules are GM opt-in world settings, off by default** (e.g. variant channeling): tables that don't use them never see extra choices (2026-10-23).
- **Prefer streamlined character-builder flow**: choices happen where PF1 already asks (the level-up window), with a one-time catch-up button on the feature for existing characters (2026-10-23).
- **Don't rewrite players' item descriptions**: they may hold flavor text; show automation in footnotes or separate items instead (2026-10-23).

## Rolls and buffs
- **Speed, furious and defending** (2026-11-16, phase 4c). **Speed uses PF1's own Haste extra attack** ("not cumulative with similar effects, such as a haste spell"), so the two can't add up; PF1 adds that attack only through its attack window, so the module adds it when the window is skipped. **Furious** reads raging from the same rage buffs as the rage-dependent defenses. **Defending is offered at the start of the wielder's turn** ("at the start of his turn before using the weapon"), in a whisper to the wielder's owner rather than a window, and **last turn's choice carries over** so it isn't a chore every round (the project lead's choice, to keep it from nagging); the first turn of a fight starts at nothing moved. Only in combat, only for equipped, identified defending weapons with an enhancement bonus.
- **Incorporeal damage** (2026-11-16, the project lead's choice to apply the rule rather than leave ghost touch as a note). Keyed off PF1's own Incorporeal condition, applied when damage is applied from a card (the card's attack tells what the source was), before resistance and DR like any halving, with the usual minimum of 1. Nonmagical: 0. Magic weapons (an enhancement bonus, the magic material, or natural attacks of a creature with DR/magic), spells, supernatural and spell-like abilities: half. Ghost touch, force, channel energy, incorporeal attackers: full. Holy water affects incorporeal undead (at half). Damage applied without an attack (typed in, bleed, burn, constrict) is left to the GM, as the source can't be told.
- **Weapon qualities that act on a hit** (2026-11-16, phase 4b) use the monster rider engine (applied when the module can tell the attack hit, else an Apply button, with an Undo). **Vorpal is never automatic** (the project lead's ruling): whether a creature has a head, or is affected by losing it, is the GM's judgment, so the GM gets a whispered message and a Sever button on the card. **Wounding's bleed adds up** ("Multiple hits from a wounding weapon increase the bleed damage"), an exception to bleed not stacking. **Thundering's deafness needs a confirmed critical hit.** **Brilliant energy** adds the target's armor and shield bonuses (its AC less its touch AC and natural armor) to the attack roll against a single target, which has the same effect as ignoring them; with several targets it's Not Applied and can be ticked by hand. Its "cannot harm undead, constructs, or objects" sets damage applied to undead and constructs to 0.
- **Magic arms and materials** (2026-11-16), modelled on the PF2e system's rune slots. **A Magic Qualities section in the Details tab** of weapons, armor and shields (not a separate window). The abilities come from Pathfinder 1e Content's special qualities pack (309 weapon, armor and shield abilities with their prices), so the module keeps no price list of its own; the ones it automates are marked. The rules are checked and shown, not enforced (a GM may allow more). **The item is named from its parts while it keeps its stock name** (its base weapon or the compendium item's name), as in PF2e; a custom name or a specific magic item keeps its own. Saving the sheet doesn't store the shown name, so the name keeps following the item. **Update price…** sets the price from the parts, asking for the plain item's price (remembered); it never changes a price on its own. **Special materials** apply PF1's own material data (price, hardness, weight) and the armor changes their rules give (mithral, darkwood, noqual, eel hide and others): PF1 stores mithral's armor check change with the opposite sign of every other material, so every value is read as the reduction the rules describe. Hit points scale with the material's hit points per inch against the usual material (adamantine's "one-third more"). **A silvered weapon keeps its metal's hardness and hit points** (the silvering is a coating). **Mithral's lighter category counts for movement and limitations, not proficiency**, as its text says. **Unidentified items show players nothing** of their abilities or the generated name; their rows in the roll window read "Unidentified quality". Abilities added for a time by spells: see *Weapon spells*; class features (divine bond, arcane pool, sacred weapon) are later work.
- **Situational bonuses, phase 2A** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choices for phase 2: read descriptions automatically with the source sentence shown and uncertain ones left to tick; a terrain choice per scene; light from the scene's lighting, tickable if unreliable). One library of situations (situational-bonuses.mjs: each recognizes its wording and checks the roll) and one note reader beside the creature-bonus reader: a context note with words beyond a bonus's plain wording is situational; recognized situations apply when they hold, others are listed Not Applied to tick. Notes about creatures stay with creature-bonuses.mjs, saves against kinds of effects with save-bonuses.mjs. **Consistency step 3**: a registry of features the module already automates (`registerHandledFeatures`, `handledFeature` in util.mjs), registered by each feature file (Studied Target, Quarry, ranged feats, two-weapon feats, Shield-Trained, rogue talents whose notes describe the target's penalties, auras, some traits); general note readers skip them. Each roll-window feature's entries are collected on their own (roll-effects.mjs): one feature's error is logged and leaves the others and the roll working.
- **Pathfinder 1e Content is required** (2026-11-18, the project lead's ruling). The spell buffs the module applies come from its buff compendium (PF1's own has few spell buffs), so it moved from recommended to required in module.json rather than the module carrying its own copies of those buffs.
- **Situational bonuses, phase 2B: a reviewed table, not live reading** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choice after the prototype). Reading descriptions while playing misread too often: about a quarter of the bonus sentences are someone else's (an ally's bonus, a penalty on the foe), many are temporary effects of an action, and some scale with level. So the descriptions are read once, ahead of time, into situational-data.mjs, and each entry is reviewed by hand: only the character's own passive bonuses, written as the notes PF1 would carry, keyed by the item's name. `util.notesOf(item)` returns an item's own notes and its table notes; every note reader uses it, so table entries behave exactly like PF1's. Entries the module can check apply on their own; the rest are listed to tick. A recognized situation with other conditions in the same note (sunder, but "against targets larger than you") stays to tick. Batch 1: feats and traits with attack, damage and combat maneuver bonuses. Batch 2: their save bonuses against kinds of effects, written in the save reader's "+N Type vs effects" form (save-bonuses.mjs reads them through notesOf too); wording that would match too widely ("ingested poisons" would count against every poison) is left out rather than made wrong. Batch 3: skill bonuses, one note per skill named; bonuses against creatures use the creature reader's "vs" form, and wording the creature words would misread ("to pose as a human") is phrased so it stays to tick. Initiative bonuses wait for an initiative reader. Critical-confirmation bonuses, AC and CMD come in later batches.
- **Consistency pass, step 1** (2026-11-17, from the consistency audit the project lead asked for). Shared helpers in util.mjs that every feature uses: `applyCondition` (a condition with a duration, lifting an existing one first because PF1 ignores a new duration; a longer existing one stays unless `extend`), `remainingSeconds`, `dealDamage` for any typed damage (DR, resistances, immunities) with `restoreDamage` for its Undo, and `rollSaveAgainst` for a save with no card that says what it's against (save-bonuses.mjs reads it: afflictions' kinds, fire). Step 2 (done): one helper each for active buffs by name (`activeBuff`), message recipients (`ownerWhisper`: owners and every GM, since a GM doesn't see a private message not addressed to them; `gmWhisper`), living creatures (`isLiving`, creature-types.mjs) and chat-card buttons (`cardButton`); the hand-built buttons with their own logic, the Undo cards (each keeps different data) and area geometry stay as they are. Step 3, with conditional bonuses phase 2: one helper each for active buffs by name, message recipients, Undo cards and card buttons, living creatures, area geometry, a recognition list of features already handled, and one note reader for creature and save bonuses.
- **Lasting areas, phase 2** (2026-11-17). Movement inside an area is noticed by comparing each inside token's position at each recheck; Spike Growth measures the feet of the straight line from where the token was at the previous recheck to where it is now that run inside the area (at least 5 feet for a creature entering), per the ruling. Grease and Sleet Storm: a failed DC 10 Acrobatics check says the creature can't move this round (the token isn't moved back); Grease then asks the Reflex save. Create Pit: depth 10 feet per two caster levels (max 30), falling damage 1d6 per 10 feet and prone; next to the pit means within one square of its area, checked at the end of each creature's turn in combat. Fog concealment is a miss-chance source by position (miss-chance.mjs's registerMissSource): 20% within 5 feet, 50% beyond, when either creature's center is in the fog or the line between them crosses it; Sleet Storm 50%. Concealment counts as such for Blind-Fight, blindsight and Improved Precise Shot.
- **Lasting areas, phase 1** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choices). The spell's PF1 template carries the area's state (flags.lastingArea: caster, caster level, DC, end time, who is inside); the active GM's client rechecks it after token or template moves (debounced) and on the caster's turns. **Removed when the spell ends** (by game time), with a note to the GM; deleting it by hand releases the same. **Entering creatures roll automatically**, with an Undo on the card. These spells' spell-card rows are skipped, so the area handles the creatures inside at the casting too (no save rolled twice). Inside means the token's center is in the template (circles and rectangles by their numbers; other shapes by the canvas). Black Tentacles' single roll per round is made at the start of the caster's turn and reused for creatures entering that round; Web's casting asks a Reflex save, moving into it the better of CMB and Escape Artist against the DC. Phase 2: Grease, Sleet Storm and Spike Growth (movement through the area, measured on the straight line of the move), Create Pit, fog concealment, Wall of Fire, and Silence centered on a moving creature.
- **Afflictions, phase 2** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choices). **Treat poison / disease**: a healer is chosen once from the clicking user's characters and makes a Heal check with every save (the rule's "every time") until Stop treating; beating the DC gives +4 competence (a formula part on the save). **Natural healing**: PF1's rest is held back so an affliction's ability damage (recorded per affliction as it's dealt) and hit point damage stay while it lasts, with a note. **Poison Use** spares only the 5% chance when applying, not the natural 1 ("you do not risk accidentally poisoning yourself when applying poison"). The dose on a weapon is the one the poison item's own use spent; only injury and contact poisons go on a weapon. The first hit the module can see delivers it to the creature hit; a natural 1 exposes the wielder and uses it up ("consumed when the weapon strikes a creature or is touched by the wielder"). Cure spells roll 1d20 + caster level against each affliction's current DC (doses included), −4 under Pernicious Poison; Delay Poison and Pernicious Poison are recognized by their buffs' names. Delay Poison stops a poison's clock (its due time moves with game time) and holds the first effect of one contracted meanwhile. Break Enchantment (DC 11 + the effect's caster level) and neutralizing a poisonous creature or object are left for later.
- **Afflictions, phase 1** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choices). The affliction is a buff on the victim holding its schedule (flags.affliction), so it shows on the sheet and deleting it ends it. Entries are read from both forms in PF1's data (a statblock's inline "Bite-injury; save Fort DC 9; frequency 1/round for 6 rounds; effect 1d2 Con; cure 1 save" and an item's labelled Type/Save/Onset/Frequency/Initial and Secondary Effect/Cure). **Without an onset, the failed contraction save is the first failed save**: its effect applies at once and it counts as the first of the frequency's saves ("1/round for 6 rounds": 6 saves in all). **The owner rolls each periodic save** from a private card; creatures no player owns roll on the active GM's client. **"/round" afflictions in combat** come due at the start of the creature's turn, once a round; everything else by game time (combat rounds advance it too). **Outside combat a "/round" affliction offers Resolve all.** **Effects apply automatically, with an Undo**: ability damage and drain, conditions with their duration (a condition with none is held while the affliction lasts and removed when it ends), hit point and nonlethal damage; anything else is shown to apply by hand. Cure: that many successes in a row (a failure resets the count); no cure entry or "magic only" never ends from saves. **Poison doses**: a failed save against the same poison (by name) while it's active adds +2 DC per earlier dose and half its total saves. Save bonuses vs poison, disease and curse count on these saves (save-bonuses.mjs reads what's being saved against). Phase 2: poisoned weapons, cure spells and Heal checks, blocking natural healing, Pernicious Poison.
- **Typed penalties** (2026-11-17, the project lead's choice: correct PF1, with a world setting to turn it off). Rules map, fundamentals: "Bonuses and penalties stack with each other, so a penalty can cancel a bonus"; "Penalties of the same type don't stack: only the worst applies." PF1 keeps the single highest change per type (its `getHighestChanges`, and on sheet totals an order-dependent "use higher value only" in `ItemChange.applyChange`). Sheet totals: `applyChange` is wrapped so a typed penalty bypasses PF1's per-type tracking and only the worst penalty of each type per target applies. Attack and damage rolls: PF1 calls its own `getHighestChanges` internally (it can't be wrapped), so in `pf1PreAttackRoll`/`pf1PreDamageRoll` typed penalties are removed from PF1's list and the worst of each type is added as its own formula part (for damage, read from the full change list, since PF1 has already filtered it, less buffs turned off in the roll window). The roll window uses the same math. Stacking types (untyped, dodge, racial, circumstance) are unchanged. The setting needs a reload.
- **Roll-window polish** (2026-11-16). On attack and damage rolls a turned-off buff is removed from PF1's own list of changes before PF1 picks the highest of each type (its `pf1PreAttackRoll` and `pf1PreDamageRoll` hooks pass that list), so the card has one line per bonus; conditions turned off stay a correction, since PF1 builds fresh condition changes for damage rolls and removing by identity would miss them. Saves, skills and ability checks keep the correction (PF1 has already totalled them). A buff the spell rows put on a creature records its caster (`flags.castBy`). When the caster is another creature and every change in the buff is a penalty, the buff is locked as a whole like a condition; a buff mixing bonuses and penalties stays the player's choice. The same creature is recognized by actor id, so an unlinked token's own spell (cast from its sheet, which names the base actor) isn't locked. When buffs leave PF1's list, the remaining correction is labeled with only what it carries (conditions turned off or on, ability buffs turned off). **Roll Bonuses audit**: its flanking, higher ground, shooting into melee, Precise Shot, Spirited Charge and Menacing bonuses are its own (this module doesn't add them to PF1 attacks); its range penalty, Improved Critical and Weapon Finesse are already coordinated (ranged.mjs, weapon-qualities.mjs, shield-trained.mjs). Bonuses it adds as formula parts aren't seen by the roll window, so a typed bonus from both modules could stack; none is known today.
- **Amulet of mighty fists and armor abilities** (2026-11-16, the project lead's choices). The amulet's abilities follow the strict reading of "so long as they can be applied to unarmed attacks": unarmed strikes deal bludgeoning damage, so keen, vorpal, seeking and other abilities limited to piercing, slashing or ranged weapons aren't offered, even for creatures whose claws slash. Its abilities reach unarmed strikes and natural weapons through the same "added from outside" path as weapon spells (shown as *From the worn amulet*), never stored on the attacks. Its enhancement bonus is read from PF1's own change on the amulet (or the "+N" in its name); PF1 already applies it to natural attacks, and the module adds the same change to unarmed strikes (attacks or weapons named "unarmed"). Against DR the amulet's bonus works as a weapon's (Paizo FAQ). A speed amulet's extra attack is given once a combat round (the FAQ refuses one per natural weapon). It's named "+N <abilities> Amulet of Mighty Fists" once abilities are chosen; a plain amulet keeps its name. **Armor abilities** apply while the armor is worn: shadow and slick as PF1 competence changes on the armor (so sheet totals include them), energy resistance (energy chosen in the section, or read from "Fire Resistance" in the name), spell resistance (the best of the wearer's and the armor's), invulnerability as DR 5/magic, all through the feature-grants path (defenses.mjs) so they don't stack with others of their kind. Champion, arrow deflection, bashing, ghost touch armor, wild and the once-a-day command abilities are later work.
- **Weapon spells** (2026-11-16, temporary qualities phase 1; the project lead chose spells before class features, as the current party casts them). The spell's buff stays on the creature (PF1 has no effects on items) and is **bound to one weapon** by a flag: its changes count for that weapon's rolls only, and what it adds (enhancement, keen, an alignment) is reported to the weapon's Magic Qualities section and the automation as *for a time*, never stored on the weapon, so its name, price and the sheet's saved data don't change. It counts toward the +10 limit ("including those from character abilities and spells"). **The weapon is the creature's only fitting one, or the first** of several (equipped weapons and attacks; never a natural weapon, except for bless weapon; keen edge only piercing or slashing), with a whispered choice to the owner and buttons on the buff, as defending's reminder. Against DR, PF1 reads only the weapon's own enhancement and alignment boxes, so the module adds magic (only magic: greater magic weapon "does not allow a weapon to bypass damage reduction aside from magic"), the chosen alignment (none on an already aligned weapon), and bless weapon's good (suppressing other alignments) and magic when every target is evil. Bless weapon's automatic confirmation against evil foes is a note on the card (PF1 still rolls the confirmation; its critical damage is on the card either way); it doesn't apply to a keen or vorpal weapon. Projectiles (50 arrows) and the warpriest, magus and paladin features are later work.
- **Weapon qualities** (2026-11-16, on-hit riders phase 4a). PF1 has no field for them, so **they're chosen on the item and read from its name when nothing is chosen** (the project lead's choice over name-only or picker-only); what's chosen replaces the name. A bane weapon's designated foe is read from the name ("Undead Bane") or chosen with the qualities, one subtype for humanoids and outsiders. The choosing moved from a separate window into the Details tab (see Magic arms and materials). Damage qualities are rows in the roll window, ticked by default and untickable, since flaming and the others work "upon command" and merciful can be suppressed; burst dice still apply when the base quality is off. Aligned and bane dice apply when every target qualifies; otherwise they're Not Applied with the reason and anyone can tick them. A die already typed into the action's damage (PF1's suggested setup) isn't added again. Keen doesn't double a range something else already expanded ("doesn't stack"). Vicious's 1d6 to the wielder follows the natural-attack rider ruling: applied when the module can tell the attack hit, else an Apply button. Phases: 4a damage, keen and alignment; 4b effects on a hit (wounding, disruption, vorpal, thundering's deafness, brilliant energy, ghost touch, seeking); 4c speed, furious, defending.
- **Burn, rend and the monster's grapple** (2026-11-10, on-hit riders phase 3). **Burn** is automatic on a melee hit the module can see, with an Undo: the fire damage (energy resistance applies), then the Reflex save; a failure sets the creature on fire for 1d4 rounds, taking the burn damage at the start of each of its turns. Each turn's message offers the full-round Reflex save, or dropping and rolling (+4, and the creature is prone). Catching fire again keeps the longer duration. Hitting a creature that has burn with a natural weapon or unarmed strike burns the attacker the same way. **Rend** is the monster's choice ("can cause"): the module counts its hits on each target during the round (on one card outside combat) with the attack its entry names, and the card that completes them offers a Rend button, usable once a round. **The grapple on later rounds**: when a grab succeeds, the module remembers it; at the start of each of the monster's turns while both are still grappled, the GM gets a card with Maintain (+4 grab, +5 for later rounds, −20 for a hold; deals the grab attack's damage and constrict, and a failure ends the grapple), Rake, Swallow Whole (a bite's hold, the target at least one size smaller) and Let go. Maintaining and swallowing are each the creature's check for the round, so the card allows one or the other. **A swallowed creature takes its damage at the start of the swallower's turn** (the rules don't say when; this gives the creature a turn to act first); a Swallowed effect shows the interior's AC and the damage needed to cut free. **Blood drain** is automatic at the end of the monster's turn while it grapples or is attached, with an Undo. **An @Use link in any attack note is a rider** even without "plus" (the stirge's touch attack). Pinning, tying up and escaping are left to the table; removing the grappled condition ends the module's record of the grapple.
- **Monster natural-attack riders** (2026-11-10, on-hit riders phase 2), read from the attack's notes ("plus grab", "plus @Use[Poison]") and the creature's own ability items. **Automatic riders** happen on every hit with no choice, by their wording: energy drain ("happens automatically when a melee or ranged attack hits"), paralysis, attach ("latches on when it hits"), bleed, poison and disease. The module applies them as soon as it can tell the attack hit (attack total against AC, natural 20 and 1, after miss chances), with an **Undo**; when it can't tell, an Apply button. **"May" riders** are the monster's choice and stay buttons: trip ("can attempt"), pull and push ("can choose to attempt"), and grab, read as optional as is common at the table, with a second "Hold (−20)" button for the body-part hold. **Poison and disease** roll only the first save for now; their schedule comes with the afflictions engine. **Where PF1's data disagrees with the rules text, the text wins**: the universal Paralysis item computes its DC from Charisma, the text from Constitution.
- **Critical feats** (2026-11-10, on-hit riders phase 1): offered on the card under each attack that threatens a critical hit, one button per feat the attacker has, chosen by the attacker after the critical is confirmed ("You can only apply the effects of one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you possess Critical Mastery"; no feat requires choosing before the roll). Once the limit is reached (two with Critical Mastery) the other buttons go. PF1's critical footnotes for the feats the module handles are hidden, so nothing is applied twice. Feats the module can't resolve (Impaling, Dirty Critical Hit, Impact Critical Shot, Dispelling, Accursed, Banishing, Entreating, Blighted, Sin-Sharing) are still offered, count toward the limit, and say to resolve them by hand. Crippling Critical lowers each of the creature's speeds by half its value when the hit lands. Phases: critical feats, then monster natural-attack riders (Trip, Grab, Paralysis, Poison), then weapon qualities and extra damage dice.
- **Auras work only on scenes in play** (2026-11-09, code review): the active scene, or one a connected user is viewing. Linked tokens left standing on last session's map gave auras from positions the creatures aren't in, and the same aura could come twice. Turning an activated aura's buff off ends it for good (the buff is removed once the unused rounds are given back), so turning it back on can't restart it without paying.
- **Auras** (2026-11-09): a curated list of auras, each checked against its own text, rather than reading aura text automatically (the wording varies too much). Each creature inside gets a linked buff, one per aura however many creatures give it (the strongest source counts), so the same aura never stacks with itself; different auras follow PF1's bonus stacking. Activated auras are switched by the bearer's buff: Use (or the feature's note) starts one, spending its rounds from the feature's own uses, and turning the buff off ends it and gives back the rounds not yet used, since "these rounds do not need to be consecutive". The design follows the PF2e system's auras, simplified: one recheck per scene on the active GM's client, shortly after tokens move or a bearer changes, creating and deleting only what differs. Readings of the text: "allies" includes the bearer (PF1 FAQ: you count as your own ally unless stated otherwise); allies and enemies are told apart by token disposition; hidden tokens neither give nor receive auras; Aura of Despair (antipaladin) and Aura of Cowardice together give −4 on saves against fear, not −6 ("does not stack"); PF1's Foretell counts its uses from the Intelligence modifier, which is corrected to the wizard level its text gives. A target's aura buff that is deactivated stays off until the creature leaves the aura (the GM's way to leave someone out).
- **Fast healing and regeneration** (2026-11-09) heal at the start of the creature's turn, in combat only, once a round (the rules say "per round"; out of combat it's left to the GM). Nonlethal damage is healed as PF1's dual-heal setting says. Damage of a regeneration's listed types applied through PF1 stops its next turn's healing. Fast healing ends with the dead condition; regeneration only notes that the creature can't die while it works (PF1 applies no death). A short line is whispered to the GM and the owner each time. Temporary hit points stay with Nevela's Automation Suite and healing abilities with PF1's buttons.
- **Saves against abilities that aren't spells** get the spell rows (2026-11-09): any action card with a save and targets (class abilities, hexes, monster and template abilities, racial traits, items) gets a row per target, rolled by clicking, with the effect read from the item's text (condition, duration, what a successful save does) and a corrections list for wrong readings, added as found. When the effect can't be read, the row still rolls the save and says to apply the effect by hand. When the condition is read but not its duration, it isn't applied: the row reminds the GM, so nothing stays on a creature by mistake. Abilities delivered by an attack get a row only for targets the attack hit. Proximity auras, poisons and diseases are left to their own engines.
- **Miss chances that are cancelled** (2026-11-09): Improved Precise Shot ("anything less than total concealment") doesn't beat displacement, whose miss chance is "as if it had total concealment"; invisibility and an unseen target are total concealment too. Faerie fire leaves PF1's plain Concealed condition alone, since it doesn't say whether it comes from darkness (which faerie fire cancels unless it's 2nd-level or higher) or from fog (which it doesn't).
- **Afflictions are tracked later, with the repeating-effects engine** (onset, periodic saves, cure count, poison doses), not as a separate feature; until then they stay with the GM (2026-10-26).
- **Evasion and improved evasion are applied automatically** on Reflex-half spell rows (no damage on a success; half on a failure with improved evasion), checking the light-or-no-armor and not-helpless conditions (2026-10-26).
- **Spells that fire several rays (scorching ray) get one attack roll and one row per ray** when automated, with the caster picking each ray's target; per-ray energy resistance and the once-per-casting damage bonuses and sneak attack follow from that (2026-10-26).
- **Area spells**: the module will work out who is inside a placed template, with line of effect from the origin, and the player can adjust the result (the template helper on the roadmap). **Spreads are treated as spheres**: tracing their path around walls is costly and rarely matters, so the players adjust. **Creatures entering or leaving a lasting emanation are not tracked yet**: deferred to the engine planned for repeating effects such as Entangle (2026-10-26).
- **Held touch spell charges get a reminder** on the caster while held (what's held, and that touching anything or casting another spell ends it), rather than being left to the player: it's easy to forget mid-fight (2026-10-26).
- **Rays and other ranged touch spells**: they follow the ranged attack rules wherever the rules leave it open (the prone, wind and willing-ally rulings below apply to every ranged touch spell, not only rays; extended 2026-10-26). A **prone caster can fire rays** (the prone restriction is about handling a ranged weapon, and a ray isn't held). **Wind penalties on ranged attacks apply to rays** (wind itself stays the GM's call, as above). **Beneficial rays still need their attack roll against willing allies**: the willing-target exception is written only for touch spells. Sneak attack with rays follows Paizo's Core Rulebook FAQ: qualifying spell attacks add it, once per casting for simultaneous rays such as scorching ray, on the ray the caster chooses (2026-10-26).
- **Ranged action economy (phase 3)**: loaded-weapon tracking is an opt-in world setting (off by default); with it on, firing an empty crossbow, sling or firearm is **blocked with a message** (as in the PF2e module). (Capping thrown full attacks without Quick Draw was dropped: PF1 already gives thrown weapons a single attack on a full attack, with or without Quick Draw.) The warning for shooting or reloading while threatened is **always on**: it's a reminder that blocks nothing (2026-10-25).
- **Ranged defenses (phase 2)**: wind is **not automated**; it is too situational and stays the GM's call, as do the feats and items that adjust it (Weathered Warrior, Storm Hunter, Silver Nocking Point…). AC against a ranged attack is shown on the attack card **only to the GM and the target's owner**: players shouldn't see enemy statistics (revised the same day from "everyone" after seeing it on a card); the outcome of a deflection is shown to all. Deflecting a ranged attack (Deflect Arrows and similar) is the defender's choice, through a button on the card (2026-10-25).
- **Spells: cantrips and 1st-level spells** (research: `docs/SPELLS-RESEARCH.md`). Each affected creature gets a row on the spell's card. **Every save is rolled by clicking**, the creature's owner or the GM, NPCs included; nothing is rolled automatically, so players keep rolling their own saves and the GM decides when an NPC rolls. **Results apply automatically** once a save fails (or a spell attack hits): the condition or buff goes on with its duration, and the row says what was applied. **Spell resistance is checked automatically** (caster level check for each target with SR, on spells that allow it): everyone sees the roll and whether it got through, only the GM sees the SR value. Immunities (mind-affecting, Hit Dice limits, creature type) are resolved when the effect applies, not shown beforehand, as with precision damage. The first phase covers the Core Rulebook, Advanced Player's Guide, Ultimate Magic and Ultimate Combat; other books follow on the same engine (2026-10-25). **Spell damage stays with PF1's damage buttons**: the row says full or half, the GM applies it. **Choices made at casting stay manual** (Command's command, splitting Magic Missiles between targets): the row's note reminds the GM (2026-10-25).
- **Ranged combat builds on Roll Bonuses** for the range-increment penalty, maximum range and shooting into melee: the module recognizes the feats, traits, spells and items that change range and feeds them into Roll Bonuses' range data, rather than applying its own penalty. Without Roll Bonuses those range changes do nothing (documented on the wiki). **No automatic cover for now**: cover stays the GM's call, and abilities that depend on it (Improved Precise Shot, Low Profile and similar) stay manual. Phases: range and Point-Blank Shot, defenses, action economy (loaded weapons and reloading as an opt-in world setting, off by default), special shots and firearms (2026-10-25). Abilities that depend on an action the module can't see (Bullseye Shot's move action, Pinpoint Targeting's standard action without moving, Opening Volley's earlier ranged hit) are offered unticked in the attack window rather than guessed. Firearm touch AC and Pinpoint Targeting show only on the GM's AC line, in line with not revealing enemy statistics to players.
- **Halved damage is halved before energy resistance; doubled damage is doubled after it** (quick-applied damage): the rules set no order and there's no Paizo FAQ; this is the common table reading and the guideline of applying effects in the order most favorable to the creature. 20 fire saved for half against resist 10 → 0 (2026-10-24).
- **Roll windows: a buff manager instead of a list in the window** (after mocking chips, folds and badges on a level-15 fighter with 14 buffs/conditions, all too busy). Up to a per-player threshold (default 4 effects) the effects show as one row of on/off chips in the roll window. Above it, the window shows one summary line ("Attack +7 · Damage +8 · from 10 sources · 6 not applied · Manage…") and **Manage…** opens a small window with a row per effect (checkbox, value, bonus type) in sections Attack only / Damage only / Attack and damage (saves and skills: one section), then **Not Applied**. Ability-score changes merge into one row per ability that expands to its sources. Changes apply to this roll only. **One-roll bonuses (Guidance, True Strike, Luck channel) always keep their checkbox in the roll window**, even above the threshold: they're used up, so it's a real choice every roll (2026-10-23).
- **Not Applied** lists bonuses that don't stack (with the higher bonus that beats them) and bonuses that don't apply to this roll (with the reason), only for rolls they could apply to. A non-stacking bonus can be ticked only after the bonus beating it is turned off, and **it never moves up on its own: the player ticks it**. "Doesn't apply" bonuses (studied target, quarry not targeted) **can be ticked by anyone**, for theater-of-mind play (2026-10-23).
- **The two-weapon fighting penalty is not listed in the roll window's buff manager**: it already has its own checkbox in the same window, and listing it twice would be clutter (2026-10-23).
- **Penalties are on by default. Ones the character chose (Fighting Defensively, Rage, TWF, their own buffs) can be unticked by anyone; imposed ones (conditions, armor check penalty, non-proficiency, debuffs cast by someone else) only by the GM** (players see a 🔒): mistakes and GM hand-waving are easy to fix, but players can't quietly drop their own conditions. If the module can't tell, a buff counts as chosen (2026-10-23).
- **Halved damage and healing never go below 1, everywhere** (PF1's ½ buttons and dialog ratio, saves for half, variant channeling): core rule "damage and hit points have a minimum of 1". Bonuses like ½ level are not damage and can be 0; resistance can still reduce damage to 0 (2026-10-23).
- **One-roll bonuses (Guidance, True Strike, Luck channel) show as a ticked box in every roll window**; unticked, the roll goes without it and the buff waits. No "arm" buttons (judged clunky). Rolls without a window use it automatically. A bonus is used up only if it ended up in the roll (2026-10-23).
- **Bit of Luck: always take the better of two d20s automatically**: taking the better roll is never worse (2026-10-22).

## Cleric / channel energy
- **A channel that doesn't happen** (cancelled, or the cleric has no token) refunds the use and deletes PF1's card, rather than leaving a card that shows the uses before the refund (2026-11-09).
- **No summary chat message after a channel**: PF1's card shows the amount and saves have their own cards; the GM request is whispered and deleted once applied (2026-10-23).
- **Variant channeling halves per the rules as written**: roll the normal dice and halve the total on PF1's own card (one number in chat), **minimum 1** ("damage and hit points have a minimum of 1") (2026-10-23).
- **Variant channeling is chosen at creation (new-cleric level-up window) or once from Channel Energy's note, then locked; GM-only reset.** The choice shows on the sheet as a "Variant Channeling (portfolio)" class feature (2026-10-23).
- **Luck variant bonus goes to every creature the channel affects**, including those already at full HP (the rules give it to "affected creatures") (2026-10-23).
- **Clerics are prepared casters**; spontaneous casting converts prepared spells (2026-10-22).

## Classes
- **Sneak attack phase 3 (feats, items, traits, fortification)**: fortification is rolled once per hit that is a critical hit or a sneak attack and negates both (the crit isn't multiplied, the sneak dice drop), shown on the card to everyone like a miss chance (a result after the attack, not a pre-attack hint). One-off negations chosen by the defender (Bracelet of Second Chances, Jingasa of the Fortunate Soldier, Fortified) get a button for the target's owner or the GM. Situational sources the module can't detect (surprise round, overgrown terrain, alchemical burn) are tickable under Not Applied with their condition (2026-10-25).
- **Sneak attack talents (phase 2)**: the rider is chosen in the roll window's "With:" dropdown, one choice for the whole window (a full attack uses it on every sneak attack). It starts on the character's last-used rider (once-a-day options never stick). Effects are applied with an **Apply** button on the attack card's footnote (GM or the attack's owner, after judging the hit), not automatically. **Bleed is fully automated**: damage at the start of the bleeding creature's turn, stopped by healing or a Heal check, a higher bleed replaces a lower one (2026-10-25).
- **Immunity to precision damage is not revealed before the attack** (as in PF2e): the roll window offers sneak attack whenever its conditions are met, the dice are typed precision damage, and the immunity removes them when the damage is applied. Innate immunities (oozes, elementals, incorporeal and amorphous creatures, gaseous form and similar) are checked at that point too. This replaces the 2026-10-24 ruling that blocked the roll-window entry (2026-10-25).
- **Sneak attack in phases** (research: `docs/SNEAK-ATTACK-RESEARCH.md`). Phase 1: dice from every class feature (PF1's formulas, summed), denied Dex / flanking / invisible-attacker detection, 30 ft range, concealment, immune creatures, Improved Uncanny Dodge, no crit multiplication, nonlethal with nonlethal weapons, Deadly Range and Sniper's Eye. Phase 2: talents, one asterisk talent per attack chosen in the roll window. Phase 3: feats, items, traits, spells, and fortification rolls (2026-10-24).
- **Sneak attack applies automatically when the module detects its conditions** (target denied Dex or flanked, in range, not concealed), shown in the roll window where it can be unticked; when not detected it's listed under Not Applied and can be ticked by hand. Before building, research every feat, talent, racial trait, trait and item that touches sneak attack (2026-10-24).
- **Shared rogue talents are offered to slayers only through the Rogue Talent talent**, not listed directly (2026-10-23).
- **Talent and feat pickers: our own searchable picker plus a "Browse all feats…" button to PF1's Feat Browser**, with a note that the browser can't check prerequisites (2026-10-23).
- **Archetype-altered features are renamed "Feature (Archetype)" and their mechanics changed**, not just annotated (2026-10-22).

- **Class feature rulings** (2026-11-04): Perfect self counts as an outsider, so banishment and dismissal work on the monk. Divine grace and unholy resilience can't coexist, and no two Charisma-to-saves abilities stack. Bardic knowledge and trapfinding don't add levels across classes unless a class says its levels stack (e.g. seeker). While raging, command-word and use-activated items work, but spell-trigger (wands, staves) and spell-completion (scrolls) items don't.

## Creatures
- **Damage reduction, energy resistance and immunities from features** (2026-11-09): a curated list of PF1's class features, bloodline powers and templates, each checked against its text, applied while the actor is prepared (nothing written to it). **Only the best DR counts**, following "the two forms of damage reduction do not stack ... the creature gets the benefit of the best damage reduction in a given situation": PF1's dialog subtracts every DR that applies, so the module keeps only the one that stops the most, for every DR including DR typed on the sheet (text that says it stacks would go on an exception list). **DR also applies to damage applied straight from the card**, worked out by PF1's own dialog math (weapon material, alignment, enhancement). **"Your energy type"** is asked once when the power is added (per bloodline for Draconic and Elemental, per copy for the Energy Resistance rage power) and can be changed from its note. **Charm and compulsion immunity**, which PF1 has no option for, are kept by the module and honored by the spell rows (the paladin's covers spells and spell-like abilities only). **Conditional grants are real:** rage-only resistance needs an active rage buff, Armor Mastery armor or a shield, and Death's Gift's DR applies only to nonlethal damage. An alchemical silver weapon counts as silver for DR/silver (PF1 lists them as separate materials).
- **A creature whose type a template changed counts only as its new type.** The original type survives as the augmented subtype ("outsider (augmented humanoid)") and matters only to effects that name it: a half-fiend human is not a humanoid for *Hold Person*. Bestiary: "A creature receives this subtype when something (usually a template) changes its original type" (2026-11-09).
- **"Subtypes that indicate kind" are species subtypes** (Zombie, Juju Zombie, Zombie Lord: "retains any subtype except for alignment subtypes and subtypes that indicate kind"): species subtypes (giant, goblinoid, human, elf, orc, reptilian and similar) are dropped; trait and origin subtypes (elemental, cold, aquatic, native, extraplanar, incorporeal, shapechanger) are kept. This is an interpretation (no Paizo FAQ), so it stays flexible: the template's footnote says which subtypes were dropped, and a GM who reads it differently adds a subtype to the template item's own Creature Subtypes field to keep it (2026-11-09).
- **Bonuses against creature types, subtypes and alignments (phase 1)** are read from the context notes PF1 and Pathfinder 1e Content already put on items ("+[[1]] Racial vs Humanoids (Orc, Goblinoid)"). Where PF1's data and the Core Rulebook disagree, PF1's data sets the bonus type (Hatred is an untyped "+1 bonus" in the book, racial in PF1's data; both stack), and gnome Defensive Training's "giant type" means the giant subtype (there is no giant type). Save notes that name both an effect and a creature ("+2 vs fear effects created by outsiders") are included. Exceptions left out of a note ("with the exception of drow") are kept in a small list of corrections, added as they are found (2026-11-09).
- **Two templates that change a creature's type**: the module asks which template sets the type, in a window that must be answered, and never guesses (2026-11-09).

## Items
- **Rulings where the item text is silent** (2026-11-04): Swirling Smoke Tattoo's 20% miss chance lasts 5 minutes (the blur duration at CL 5). Kybwa-ka War Mask's fear lasts 1 round. Regression Mine's DC 15 save each round is a Will save. Righteous Medal of Vigor's swift-action +2 Constitution (1 minute) has no daily limit.

## Process
- **Testing budget**: targeted test batches while building, one full suite run before each commit, time-boxed flake hunting (2026-10-23).
- **Performance review every 10 features** (2026-10-22).
- **Read the full rules text and propose a design before building** a new automation (standing). The design must include a rules check: who, when, whose choice, duration, stacking/bonus type, rounding and minimums, and the general rules that apply (2026-10-23).
