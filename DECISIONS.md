# PF1e Revamp – Design decisions

Rulings made with the project lead, so later changes don't quietly undo them. Read the entries for a
feature before changing it. Newest first within each section. Format: **decision**: why (date).

## General UX
- **What players see about enemies**: random rolls (miss chances, fortification) are shown on cards to everyone, since they're chance outcomes; fixed enemy statistics (AC, damage reduction, immunities) are not: they show only to the GM and the creature's owner, or take effect without being announced (precision immunity) (2026-10-25).
- **Raging Song keeps the plain "no rounds left" message**, also when the daily rounds were never filled (a new feature before the first rest); no separate "rest first" wording (2026-10-24).
- **Every window the module opens names the creature it acts for** (`util.titleFor`: "Title – Name", PF1's style), and **every control that can't act says why** (tooltip or message), never silently doing nothing. PF1's card save button rolls for the selected token: warn (don't block) when that isn't a card target. From the PF2e study, where a save rolled for the wrong creature unnoticed (2026-10-23).
- **Any dropdown or list the module adds with more than 10 options gets a search box** (`util.addSearch` / `searchSelects`): long lists are painful to scroll (2026-10-23).
- **Every picker option has a 📖 icon that opens the full compendium entry**: players need to read an option before choosing it (2026-10-23).
- **Optional rules are GM opt-in world settings, off by default** (e.g. variant channeling): tables that don't use them never see extra choices (2026-10-23).
- **Prefer streamlined character-builder flow**: choices happen where PF1 already asks (the level-up window), with a one-time catch-up button on the feature for existing characters (2026-10-23).
- **Don't rewrite players' item descriptions**: they may hold flavor text; show automation in footnotes or separate items instead (2026-10-23).

## Rolls and buffs
- **Auras** (2026-11-09): a curated list of auras, each checked against its own text, rather than reading aura text automatically (the wording varies too much). Each creature inside gets a linked buff, one per aura however many creatures give it (the strongest source counts), so the same aura never stacks with itself; different auras follow PF1's bonus stacking. Activated auras are switched by the bearer's buff: Use (or the feature's note) starts one, spending its rounds from the feature's own uses, and turning the buff off ends it and gives back the rounds not yet used, since "these rounds do not need to be consecutive". The design follows the PF2e system's auras, simplified: one recheck per scene on the active GM's client, shortly after tokens move or a bearer changes, creating and deleting only what differs. Readings of the text: "allies" includes the bearer (PF1 FAQ: you count as your own ally unless stated otherwise); allies and enemies are told apart by token disposition; hidden tokens neither give nor receive auras; Aura of Despair (antipaladin) and Aura of Cowardice together give −4 on saves against fear, not −6 ("does not stack"); PF1's Foretell counts its uses from the Intelligence modifier, which is corrected to the wizard level its text gives. A target's aura buff that is deactivated stays off until the creature leaves the aura (the GM's way to leave someone out).
- **Fast healing and regeneration** (2026-11-09) heal at the start of the creature's turn, in combat only, once a round (the rules say "per round"; out of combat it's left to the GM). Nonlethal damage is healed as PF1's dual-heal setting says. Damage of a regeneration's listed types applied through PF1 stops its next turn's healing. Fast healing ends with the dead condition; regeneration only notes that the creature can't die while it works (PF1 applies no death). A short line is whispered to the GM and the owner each time. Temporary hit points stay with Nevela's Automation Suite and healing abilities with PF1's buttons.
- **Saves against abilities that aren't spells** get the spell rows (2026-11-09): any action card with a save and targets (class abilities, hexes, monster and template abilities, racial traits, items) gets a row per target, rolled by clicking, with the effect read from the item's text (condition, duration, what a successful save does) and a corrections list for wrong readings, added as found. When the effect can't be read, the row still rolls the save and says to apply the effect by hand. When the condition is read but not its duration, it isn't applied: the row reminds the GM, so nothing stays on a creature by mistake. Abilities delivered by an attack get a row only for targets the attack hit. Proximity auras, poisons and diseases are left to their own engines.
- **Miss chances that are cancelled** (2026-11-09): Improved Precise Shot ("anything less than total concealment") doesn't beat displacement, whose miss chance is "as if it had total concealment"; invisibility and an unseen target are total concealment too. Faerie fire leaves PF1's plain Concealed condition alone, since it doesn't say whether it comes from darkness (which faerie fire cancels unless it's 2nd-level or higher) or from fog (which it doesn't).
- **Afflictions are tracked later, with the repeating-effects engine** (onset, periodic saves, cure count, poison doses), not as a separate feature; until then they stay with the GM (2026-10-26).
- **Evasion and improved evasion are applied automatically** on Reflex-half spell rows (no damage on a success; half on a failure with improved evasion), checking the light-or-no-armor and not-helpless conditions (2026-10-26).
- **Spells that fire several rays (scorching ray) get one attack roll and one row per ray** when automated, with the caster picking each ray's target; per-ray energy resistance and the once-per-casting damage bonuses and sneak attack follow from that (2026-10-26).
- **Area spells**: the module will work out who is inside a placed template, with line of effect from the origin, and the player can adjust the result (the template helper on the roadmap). **Spreads are treated as spheres**: tracing their path around walls is costly and rarely matters, so the players adjust. **Creatures entering or leaving a lasting emanation are not tracked yet**: deferred to the engine planned for repeating effects such as Entangle (2026-10-26).
- **Held touch spell charges get a reminder** on the caster while held (what's held, and that touching anything or casting another spell ends it), rather than being left to the player: it's easy to forget mid-fight (2026-10-26).
- **Rays and other ranged touch spells**: they follow the ranged attack rules wherever the rules leave it open (the prone, wind and willing-ally rulings below apply to every ranged touch spell, not only rays; extended 2026-10-26). A **prone caster can fire rays** (the prone restriction is about handling a ranged weapon, and a ray isn't held). **Wind penalties on ranged attacks apply to rays** (wind itself stays the GM's call, as above). **Beneficial rays still need their attack roll against willing allies**: the willing-target exception is written only for touch spells. Sneak attack with rays follows Paizo's Core Rulebook FAQ: qualifying spell attacks add it, once per casting for simultaneous rays such as scorching ray, on the ray the caster chooses (2026-10-26).
- **Ranged action economy (phase 3)**: loaded-weapon tracking is an opt-in world setting (off by default); with it on, firing an empty crossbow, sling or firearm is **blocked with a message** (as in the PF2e module). (Capping thrown full attacks without Quick Draw was dropped: PF1 already gives thrown weapons a single attack on a full attack, with or without Quick Draw.) The warning for shooting or reloading while threatened is **always on**: it's a reminder that blocks nothing (2026-10-25).
- **Ranged defenses (phase 2)**: wind is **not automated**; it is too situational and stays the GM's call, as do the feats and items that adjust it (Weathered Warrior, Storm Hunter, Silver Nocking Point…). AC against a ranged attack is shown on the attack card **only to the GM and the target's owner**: players shouldn't see enemy statistics (revised the same day from "everyone" after seeing it on a card); the outcome of a deflection is shown to all. Deflecting a ranged attack (Deflect Arrows and similar) is the defender's choice, through a button on the card (2026-10-25).
- **Spells: cantrips and 1st-level spells** (research: `docs/SPELLS-RESEARCH.md`). Each affected creature gets a row on the spell's card. **Every save is rolled by clicking**, the creature's owner or the GM, NPCs included; nothing is rolled automatically, so players keep rolling their own saves and the GM decides when an NPC rolls. **Results apply automatically** once a save fails (or a spell attack hits): the condition or buff goes on with its duration, and the row says what was applied. **Spell resistance is checked automatically** (caster level check for each target with SR, on spells that allow it): everyone sees the roll and whether it got through, only the GM sees the SR value. Immunities (mind-affecting, Hit Dice limits, creature type) are resolved when the effect applies, not shown beforehand, as with precision damage. The first phase covers the Core Rulebook, Advanced Player's Guide, Ultimate Magic and Ultimate Combat; other books follow on the same engine (2026-10-25). **Spell damage stays with PF1's damage buttons**: the row says full or half, the GM applies it. **Choices made at casting stay manual** (Command's command, splitting Magic Missiles between targets): the row's note reminds the GM (2026-10-25).
- **Ranged combat builds on Roll Bonuses** for the range-increment penalty, maximum range and shooting into melee: the module recognizes the feats, traits, spells and items that change range and feeds them into Roll Bonuses' range data, rather than applying its own penalty. Without Roll Bonuses those range changes do nothing (documented on the wiki). **No automatic cover for now**: cover stays the GM's call, and abilities that depend on it (Improved Precise Shot, Low Profile and similar) stay manual. Phases: range and Point-Blank Shot, defenses, action economy (loaded weapons and reloading as an opt-in world setting, off by default), special shots and firearms (2026-10-25). Abilities that depend on an action the module can't see (Bullseye Shot's move action, Pinpoint Targeting's standard action without moving, Opening Volley's earlier ranged hit) are offered unticked in the attack window rather than guessed. Firearm touch AC and Pinpoint Targeting show only on the GM's AC line, in line with not revealing enemy statistics to players.
- **Halved damage is halved before energy resistance; doubled damage is doubled after it** (quick-applied damage): the rules set no order and there's no Paizo FAQ; this is the common table reading and the guideline of applying effects in the order most favorable to the creature. 20 fire saved for half against resist 10 → 0 (2026-10-24).
- **Roll windows: a buff manager instead of a list in the window** (after mocking chips, folds and badges on a level-15 fighter with 14 buffs/conditions, all too busy). Up to a per-player threshold (default 4 effects) the effects show as one row of on/off chips in the roll window. Above it, the window shows one summary line ("Attack +7 · Damage +8 · from 10 sources · 6 not applied · Manage…") and **Manage…** opens a small window with a row per effect (checkbox, value, bonus type) in sections Attack only / Damage only / Attack and damage (saves and skills: one section), then **Not Applied**. Ability-score changes merge into one row per ability that expands to its sources. Changes apply to this roll only. **One-roll bonuses (Guidance, True Strike, Luck channel) always keep their checkbox in the roll window**, even above the threshold: they're used up, so it's a real choice every roll (2026-10-23).
- **Not Applied** lists bonuses that don't stack (with the higher bonus that beats them) and bonuses that don't apply to this roll (with the reason), only for rolls they could apply to. A non-stacking bonus can be ticked only after the bonus beating it is turned off, and **it never moves up on its own: the player ticks it**. "Doesn't apply" bonuses (studied target, quarry not targeted) **can be ticked by anyone**, for theater-of-mind play (2026-10-23).
- **The two-weapon fighting penalty is not listed in the roll window's buff manager**: it already has its own checkbox in the same window, and listing it twice would be clutter (2026-10-23).
- **Penalties are on by default. Ones the character chose (Fighting Defensively, Rage, TWF, their own buffs) can be unticked by anyone; imposed ones (conditions, armor check penalty, non-proficiency, debuffs cast by someone else) only by the GM** (players see a 🔒): mistakes and GM hand-waving are easy to fix, but players can't quietly drop their own conditions. If the module can't tell, a buff counts as chosen (2026-10-23).
- **Halved damage and healing never go below 1, everywhere** (PF1's ½ buttons and dialog ratio, saves for half, variant channeling): core rule "damage and hit points have a minimum of 1". Bonuses like ½ level are not damage and can be 0; resistance can still reduce damage to 0 (2026-10-23).
- **One-roll bonuses (Guidance, True Strike, Luck channel) show as a ticked box in every roll window**; unticked, the roll goes without it and the buff waits. No "arm" buttons (judged clunky). Rolls without a window use it automatically. A bonus is used up only if it ended up in the roll (2026-10-23).
- **Bit of Luck: always take the better of two d20s automatically**: taking the better roll is never worse (2026-10-22).

## Cleric / channel energy
- **A channel that doesn't happen** (cancelled, or the cleric has no token) refunds the use and deletes PF1's card, rather than leaving a card that shows the uses before the refund (2026-11-09).
- **No summary chat message after a channel**: PF1's card shows the amount and saves have their own cards; the GM request is whispered and deleted once applied (2026-10-23).
- **Variant channeling halves per the rules as written**: roll the normal dice and halve the total on PF1's own card (one number in chat), **minimum 1** ("damage and hit points have a minimum of 1") (2026-10-23).
- **Variant channeling is chosen at creation (new-cleric level-up window) or once from Channel Energy's note, then locked; GM-only reset.** The choice shows on the sheet as a "Variant Channeling (portfolio)" class feature (2026-10-23).
- **Luck variant bonus goes to every creature the channel affects**, including those already at full HP (the rules give it to "affected creatures") (2026-10-23).
- **Clerics are prepared casters**; spontaneous casting converts prepared spells (2026-10-22).

## Classes
- **Sneak attack phase 3 (feats, items, traits, fortification)**: fortification is rolled once per hit that is a critical hit or a sneak attack and negates both (the crit isn't multiplied, the sneak dice drop), shown on the card to everyone like a miss chance (a result after the attack, not a pre-attack hint). One-off negations chosen by the defender (Bracelet of Second Chances, Jingasa of the Fortunate Soldier, Fortified) get a button for the target's owner or the GM. Situational sources the module can't detect (surprise round, overgrown terrain, alchemical burn) are tickable under Not Applied with their condition (2026-10-25).
- **Sneak attack talents (phase 2)**: the rider is chosen in the roll window's "With:" dropdown, one choice for the whole window (a full attack uses it on every sneak attack). It starts on the character's last-used rider (once-a-day options never stick). Effects are applied with an **Apply** button on the attack card's footnote (GM or the attack's owner, after judging the hit), not automatically. **Bleed is fully automated**: damage at the start of the bleeding creature's turn, stopped by healing or a Heal check, a higher bleed replaces a lower one (2026-10-25).
- **Immunity to precision damage is not revealed before the attack** (as in PF2e): the roll window offers sneak attack whenever its conditions are met, the dice are typed precision damage, and the immunity removes them when the damage is applied. Innate immunities (oozes, elementals, incorporeal and amorphous creatures, gaseous form and similar) are checked at that point too. This replaces the 2026-10-24 ruling that blocked the roll-window entry (2026-10-25).
- **Sneak attack in phases** (research: `docs/SNEAK-ATTACK-RESEARCH.md`). Phase 1: dice from every class feature (PF1's formulas, summed), denied Dex / flanking / invisible-attacker detection, 30 ft range, concealment, immune creatures, Improved Uncanny Dodge, no crit multiplication, nonlethal with nonlethal weapons, Deadly Range and Sniper's Eye. Phase 2: talents, one asterisk talent per attack chosen in the roll window. Phase 3: feats, items, traits, spells, and fortification rolls (2026-10-24).
- **Sneak attack applies automatically when the module detects its conditions** (target denied Dex or flanked, in range, not concealed), shown in the roll window where it can be unticked; when not detected it's listed under Not Applied and can be ticked by hand. Before building, research every feat, talent, racial trait, trait and item that touches sneak attack (2026-10-24).
- **Shared rogue talents are offered to slayers only through the Rogue Talent talent**, not listed directly (2026-10-23).
- **Talent and feat pickers: our own searchable picker plus a "Browse all feats…" button to PF1's Feat Browser**, with a note that the browser can't check prerequisites (2026-10-23).
- **Archetype-altered features are renamed "Feature (Archetype)" and their mechanics changed**, not just annotated (2026-10-22).

- **Class feature rulings** (2026-11-04): Perfect self counts as an outsider, so banishment and dismissal work on the monk. Divine grace and unholy resilience can't coexist, and no two Charisma-to-saves abilities stack. Bardic knowledge and trapfinding don't add levels across classes unless a class says its levels stack (e.g. seeker). While raging, command-word and use-activated items work, but spell-trigger (wands, staves) and spell-completion (scrolls) items don't.

## Creatures
- **A creature whose type a template changed counts only as its new type.** The original type survives as the augmented subtype ("outsider (augmented humanoid)") and matters only to effects that name it: a half-fiend human is not a humanoid for *Hold Person*. Bestiary: "A creature receives this subtype when something (usually a template) changes its original type" (2026-11-09).
- **"Subtypes that indicate kind" are species subtypes** (Zombie, Juju Zombie, Zombie Lord: "retains any subtype except for alignment subtypes and subtypes that indicate kind"): species subtypes (giant, goblinoid, human, elf, orc, reptilian and similar) are dropped; trait and origin subtypes (elemental, cold, aquatic, native, extraplanar, incorporeal, shapechanger) are kept. This is an interpretation (no Paizo FAQ), so it stays flexible: the template's footnote says which subtypes were dropped, and a GM who reads it differently adds a subtype to the template item's own Creature Subtypes field to keep it (2026-11-09).
- **Bonuses against creature types, subtypes and alignments (phase 1)** are read from the context notes PF1 and Pathfinder 1e Content already put on items ("+[[1]] Racial vs Humanoids (Orc, Goblinoid)"). Where PF1's data and the Core Rulebook disagree, PF1's data sets the bonus type (Hatred is an untyped "+1 bonus" in the book, racial in PF1's data; both stack), and gnome Defensive Training's "giant type" means the giant subtype (there is no giant type). Save notes that name both an effect and a creature ("+2 vs fear effects created by outsiders") are included. Exceptions left out of a note ("with the exception of drow") are kept in a small list of corrections, added as they are found (2026-11-09).
- **Two templates that change a creature's type**: the module asks which template sets the type, in a window that must be answered, and never guesses (2026-11-09).

## Items
- **Rulings where the item text is silent** (2026-11-04): Swirling Smoke Tattoo's 20% miss chance lasts 5 minutes (the blur duration at CL 5). Kybwa-ka War Mask's fear lasts 1 round. Regression Mine's DC 15 save each round is a Will save. Righteous Medal of Vigor's swift-action +2 Constitution (1 minute) has no daily limit.

## Process
- **Testing budget**: targeted test batches while building, one full suite run before each commit, time-boxed flake hunting (2026-10-23).
- **Performance review every 10 features** (2026-10-22).
- **Read the full rules text and propose a design before building** a new automation (standing). The design must include a rules check: who, when, whose choice, duration, stacking/bonus type, rounding and minimums, and the general rules that apply (2026-10-23).
